# Target Facebook Groups for ImNotAnAttorney

## Strategy Overview

Facebook skews older than TikTok/Instagram. The INNA audience on Facebook is primarily:
- Parents and family members of defendants (searching for help for their loved ones)
- Defendants aged 30-55 who use Facebook as their primary social platform
- People in the criminal justice system looking for community and information

**Approach:** Provide genuine value. Never sell in groups. Build trust through education. Drive traffic via profile link and first-comment method only after establishing credibility (minimum 2 weeks of pure value posts before any link sharing).

## Engagement Calendar

**Week 1-2:** Pure value. Respond to other people's posts. Share educational comments. Build recognition. Zero self-promotion.

**Week 3-4:** Start posting original content (Know Your Rights, Question of the Day). No links. No mentions of INNA. Just value.

**Week 5-6:** Begin adding blog links in first comments on your own posts. Profile should link to imnotanattorney.com by now.

**Week 7+:** Consistent posting schedule. Mix of all content types. Respond to every comment on your posts. Build reputation as the "person who actually knows about defendant rights."

## Rules of Engagement

1. NEVER sell in groups. Ever. No product mentions. No pricing.
2. NEVER give legal advice. Always frame as "questions to ask your attorney."
3. ALWAYS add the disclaimer: "This is information, not legal advice."
4. Respond to people in crisis with genuine empathy first, information second.
5. If someone asks "what should I do?", respond with "here are questions you might want to ask your attorney" never "you should do X."
6. Build relationships with group admins. Be their favorite contributor.
7. Track which groups drive the most profile visits and website traffic.
