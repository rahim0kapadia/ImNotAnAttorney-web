### Post Content

How do you know if your defense is on track, or falling behind?

Most defendants have no frame of reference. You do not know what "normal" looks like. You do not know if three continuances is fine or a red flag. You do not know if your attorney's silence means nothing is wrong or everything is wrong.

We built a free quiz that gives you a score based on where your case actually stands.

It asks about your case stage, what has been filed, how your attorney is communicating, and what you do and do not know about your own evidence.

It takes about 3 minutes. You get a score and a breakdown of what milestones should have been hit by now, and which ones have not.

No email required. No sales pitch. Just a reality check on where your defense stands.

It is not legal advice. It is a benchmark. A way to know whether the right questions are being asked and the right work is being done.

### Link Strategy

First comment (pinned): "Here's the quiz: imnotanattorney.com/score, takes about 3 minutes. No email needed. Just tells you where your defense stands."

Do NOT put the link in the post body. Use the first-comment method for organic reach protection.

### Engagement Strategy

After posting, monitor for comments. People will share their scores or ask about what the results mean. Respond to every one with encouragement: "That score gives you a starting point. The milestones it flagged are the ones to bring up with your attorney." Do NOT interpret individual scores as legal assessments.

### UPL Compliance Note

The Defense Milestone Score is an informational tool, not legal analysis. The quiz asks about case status indicators and provides general benchmarks. If someone asks "what does my score mean for my case?" respond with: "It highlights the milestones that should have been reached by now based on your case stage. The next step is to bring those specific milestones to your attorney and ask why they have or have not been addressed."
