### Post Content

Question of the day:

Has your attorney filed a single motion in your case?

Not a continuance. Not an appearance entry. An actual motion — a motion to suppress evidence, a motion to compel discovery, a motion to dismiss.

If you don't know the answer, that's the problem.

Here's how to check: Most jurisdictions have online court dockets. Search "[your county] clerk of court" and look up your case number. You can see every document that has been filed.

If the only things on the docket are appearance filings and continuance requests... your attorney hasn't been working. They've been postponing.

A motion is how your attorney challenges the prosecution's case. No motions means no challenge. No challenge means you're being processed, not defended.

Have you checked your docket? What did you find?

(This is information, not legal advice.)
