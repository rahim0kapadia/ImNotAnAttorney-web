## Facebook Post: Question of the Day — Prosecution Weaknesses

**Type**: Text post (colored background)
**Distribution**: Groups (rotate across 3-4 groups, one per day)

**Format**: Colored text background (burgundy)

### Post Content

Every single case has weaknesses. Every one.

The prosecution's case against you is not perfect. There are inconsistencies in police reports. There are gaps in evidence chains. There are witnesses who might not hold up under cross-examination. There are procedures that may not have been followed correctly.

Has your attorney told you what those weaknesses are?

Not just "we have a good case." Not just "I think we can work something out."

Specific weaknesses. Like:

- "The officer's report says the stop happened at 11:15 PM but the body camera shows 11:42 PM."
- "The field test came back positive but the lab test hasn't been completed."
- "The warrant application contains information that may have been exaggerated."
- "The key witness has a credibility issue that we can challenge."

If your attorney has not walked you through the specific weaknesses in the prosecution's case — ask them. Tomorrow.

"What are the three biggest weaknesses in the State's case against me?"

Their answer — or their inability to give one — tells you whether they've actually done the work.

What has your attorney told you about the weaknesses in your case?

(This is information, not legal advice.)
