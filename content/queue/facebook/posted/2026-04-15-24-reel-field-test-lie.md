### Caption

Roadside field tests can show a false positive for caffeine, certain medications, baking soda, and vitamins.

That cheap chemical kit gives the officer probable cause to arrest you. But it is NOT the actual evidence. The lab test is.

Two questions if you're facing drug charges:
1. Has the substance been tested in a laboratory?
2. Do the lab results match the charges?

People have pled guilty to possession of substances that turned out to not be illegal. Because nobody checked.

(This is information, not legal advice.)
