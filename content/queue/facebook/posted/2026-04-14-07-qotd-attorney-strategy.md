### Post Content

Quick question:

If I asked your attorney right now, "What is your theory of defense in this case?", could they answer?

Not "we're working on it." Not "trust the process." Not "I'll handle it."

An actual, specific answer. Something like: "The traffic stop was unconstitutional because the officer lacked reasonable suspicion, so we're filing a motion to suppress everything found after the stop."

THAT is a theory of defense.

"Don't worry about it" is not.

If your attorney cannot articulate a specific strategy for your case, what they plan to challenge, what they plan to argue, what weaknesses they've found in the prosecution's evidence, then ask yourself: do they have one?

The next time you talk to your attorney, ask them this exact question:

"What is your theory of defense?"

Write down the answer word for word. If they can't give you one, that tells you everything.

Has your attorney ever explained their actual strategy to you?

(This is information, not legal advice.)
