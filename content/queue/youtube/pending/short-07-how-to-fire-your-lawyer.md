### Full Script

**[0:00-0:04] HOOK — TEXT ON SCREEN: "How to fire your lawyer without killing your case"**
[Direct to camera, calm but serious]
"You want to fire your attorney. Here's exactly how to do it without hurting your case. Do not skip any of these steps."

**[0:04-0:14] STEP 1 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Step 1: Line up new counsel FIRST"**
"Step one — and this is the most important — line up new counsel BEFORE you fire your current attorney. Do not leave yourself without representation. Consult two or three new attorneys. Explain the situation honestly."

**[0:14-0:23] STEP 2 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Step 2: Request your complete case file"**
"Step two. Request your complete case file. All discovery. All motions. All correspondence. All court filings. You have a legal right to this. Get it in writing: 'I am requesting a complete copy of my case file.' Do this before you notify them."

**[0:23-0:31] STEP 3 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Step 3: Handle the money"**
"Step three. Review your retainer agreement. How much was earned versus unearned? In most states, your attorney must refund the unearned portion. If they refuse, your state bar has a fee dispute resolution program."

**[0:31-0:39] STEP 4 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Step 4: Notify in WRITING"**
"Step four. Send a formal written notice. Email works but follow up with a letter. Keep it professional. State the facts. No accusations. Just: 'I am terminating your representation effective this date.'"

**[0:39-0:46] STEP 5 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Step 5: New attorney files substitution"**
"Step five. Your new attorney files a substitution of counsel with the court. Judge approves it. Old attorney is off. New attorney is on. It's routine — judges see this constantly."

**[0:46-0:50] THE MONEY OBJECTION — TEXT ON SCREEN: "But I already paid $10K..."**
"And if you're thinking 'but I already paid ten thousand dollars' — that money is spent whether you stay or go. A new attorney who actually works your case is worth more than a bad attorney you overpaid."
