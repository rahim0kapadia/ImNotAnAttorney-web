**[0:05-0:18] QUESTION 1 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q1: What specific work have you done since our last court date?"**
"Number one: 'What specific work have you done on my case since our last court date?' Not 'how's it going.' Not 'any updates.' SPECIFIC work. Motions drafted. Witnesses contacted. Discovery reviewed. A good attorney will have a real answer. A coasting attorney will give you a vague one."

**[0:18-0:31] QUESTION 2 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q2: What are the 3 biggest weaknesses in the prosecution's case?"**
"Number two: 'What are the three biggest weaknesses in the prosecution's case?' Every case has weaknesses. Every single one. If your attorney can't articulate what they are — they either haven't looked, or they don't want you to know they haven't looked."

**[0:31-0:43] QUESTION 3 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q3: If this were YOUR case, what would you do?"**
"Number three: 'If this were your case — not your client's, YOUR case — what would you do?' Then stop talking. Watch their face. A good attorney will give you a direct, honest answer. A bad attorney will deflect."

**[0:43-0:52] CTA — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Your case. Your money. Your freedom."**
"Your attorney works for you. Not the other way around. You're paying them to defend your freedom, and you have every right to know what they're doing with your money and your life. Know What They Know. Link in bio."
