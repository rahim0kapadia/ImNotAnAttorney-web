### Full Script

**[0:00-0:05] HOOK — TEXT ON SCREEN: "You have the RIGHT to see the evidence against you"**
[Direct to camera, firm tone]
"The prosecution has a file on you. It contains every piece of evidence they plan to use to convict you. You have a constitutional right to see it. Most defendants never do."

**[0:05-0:07] TRANSITION — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Here's what to ask for."**
"Here's what you should be reading."

**[0:07-0:16] DOC 1 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#1: Police reports"**
"Number one: police reports. Every officer who was on scene wrote one. Do their stories match? Are there contradictions between them? You were THERE — you'll catch things your attorney might miss."

**[0:16-0:24] DOC 2 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#2: Lab results + chain of custody"**
"Number two: lab results and chain of custody records. In drug cases, this is everything. Was the substance actually tested by a lab — or just field-tested? Who handled it between the scene and the lab? Any gap in that chain is a defense."

**[0:24-0:32] DOC 3 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#3: Body cam + dash cam footage"**
"Number three: body camera and dash cam footage. If it exists, you're entitled to see it. And if the officer's written report says one thing but the footage shows another — that's reasonable doubt."

**[0:32-0:40] DOC 4 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#4: Witness statements"**
"Number four: witness statements. Every person who gave a statement to police. Do those statements match what actually happened? Were there witnesses who were never interviewed? What's missing is just as important as what's there."

**[0:40-0:48] DOC 5 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#5: Search warrant and probable cause"**
"Number five: the search warrant. What was the probable cause? Was it specific enough? Was the search executed within the scope of the warrant? If any of this is defective, the evidence it produced can be suppressed."

**[0:48-0:56] CTA — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Ask your attorney: Can I see my discovery?"**
"You don't need a law degree. You just need one sentence: 'Can I see my discovery?' If your attorney says no — that's information. We research. You ask. Link in bio."
