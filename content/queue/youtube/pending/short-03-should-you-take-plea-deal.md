### Full Script

**[0:00-0:05] HOOK — TEXT ON SCREEN: "97% of federal cases end in plea deals"**
[Direct to camera, serious tone]
"Ninety-seven percent of federal criminal cases end in plea bargains. Most defendants make that decision with almost no information. Do not be one of them."

**[0:05-0:11] THE PROBLEM — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Greet 'em. Meet 'em. Plead 'em."**
"There's a phrase in the legal profession: greet 'em, meet 'em, plead 'em. It describes attorneys who push whatever the State offers without doing the work first. The question isn't whether pleas are bad. Sometimes they're the smart move. The question is — are you making this decision with full information?"

**[0:11-0:22] QUESTION 1 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q1: Has discovery been fully reviewed?"**
"Before you sign anything, ask your attorney: have you received and reviewed ALL discovery? Many plea deals are accepted before the defense has even seen the complete evidence. You wouldn't buy a house without an inspection. Don't plead guilty without seeing what they've got."

**[0:22-0:33] QUESTION 2 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q2: What are the weaknesses in their case?"**
"Question two: what are the three biggest weaknesses in the State's case? Every case has weaknesses. Every single one. If your attorney can't name them, they either haven't looked — or they don't want to."

**[0:33-0:44] QUESTION 3 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Q3: What motions have you filed?"**
"Question three: what motions have you filed? If evidence gets suppressed, the whole case changes. Sometimes it collapses entirely. Yet many attorneys accept pleas without filing a single suppression motion. Ask: have you even tried?"

**[0:44-0:51] THE KEY — TEXT ON SCREEN: "The recommendation comes AFTER the work"**
"Here's the rule: the plea recommendation should come AFTER the investigation. Not before it. If your attorney is pushing the plea before they've done the work — that's not strategy. That's convenience."

**[0:51-0:58] CTA — TEXT ON SCREEN: "imnotanattorney.com"**
