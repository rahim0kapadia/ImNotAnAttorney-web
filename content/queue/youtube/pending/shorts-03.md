## Script
[0:00-0:04] HOOK:
[Direct to camera, leaning in]
"Your attorney says take the plea. Before you sign anything, ask these three questions. Your answer to each one determines whether you're making this decision with information or with fear."

[0:04-0:18] QUESTION 1:
[TEXT ON SCREEN: "#1: Have you received and reviewed ALL discovery?"]
"Number one. Ask your attorney: have you received and reviewed ALL discovery? Many plea deals are offered and accepted before the defense has even seen the complete evidence. You wouldn't buy a house without an inspection. Don't plead guilty without seeing everything they have on you."

[0:18-0:32] QUESTION 2:
[TEXT ON SCREEN: "#2: What are the 3 biggest weaknesses in the prosecution's case?"]
"Number two. What are the three biggest weaknesses in the prosecution's case? Every case has weaknesses. Every single one. If your attorney recommends a plea and can't name three weaknesses in the State's case — they haven't done the analysis required to give you an informed recommendation."

[0:32-0:46] QUESTION 3:
[TEXT ON SCREEN: "#3: What are ALL the collateral consequences?"]
"Number three. What are ALL the collateral consequences of this plea? Not just the sentence. Employment. Housing. Immigration. Professional licenses. Voting rights. Child custody. A guilty plea doesn't just mean probation or prison. It means everything that comes after. Your attorney should walk you through every one of these before you sign."
