[PATTERN INTERRUPT: screen goes dark, text fades in slowly]
[Text on screen: "THE TRIAL PENALTY"]
"This is something the system never puts in writing. If you exercise your constitutional right to a jury trial and lose, you will almost certainly receive a harsher sentence than if you had taken the plea deal."

"Number four: investigate independently."
[Text on screen: "4. Independent investigation"]
