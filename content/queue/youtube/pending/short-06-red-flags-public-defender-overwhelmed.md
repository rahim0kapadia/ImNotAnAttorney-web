### Full Script

**[0:00-0:05] HOOK, TEXT ON SCREEN: "7.5 minutes per week"**
[Direct to camera, measured but urgent]
"The ABA recommends 150 felony cases per attorney per year. Most public defenders carry 300 to 500. Do the math. That's seven and a half minutes per client per week. That's not enough time to read a police report."

**[0:05-0:09] FRAMING, TEXT ON SCREEN: "Not bad people. Bad math."**
"This isn't about bad attorneys. Many public defenders are genuinely skilled. It's about a system that's chronically underfunded. But you still need to know where you stand."

**[0:09-0:18] RED FLAG 1, TEXT ON SCREEN: "#1: You met them at arraignment"**
"Red flag one. You met your attorney for the first time at arraignment, and they introduced themselves 30 seconds before your name was called. That's standard with public defenders. It's also a sign they haven't had time to review your case."

**[0:18-0:27] RED FLAG 2, TEXT ON SCREEN: "#2: They mix up your facts"**
"Red flag two. They confuse your case with someone else's. They call you by the wrong name. They forget your charges. When an attorney has 400 clients, your file is one of 400. Details slip."

**[0:27-0:36] RED FLAG 3, TEXT ON SCREEN: "#3: Plea first, investigate never"**
"Red flag three. They push the plea offer without filing a single motion. Not because the plea is the right call, but because they don't have time to investigate the alternative."

**[0:36-0:45] WHAT TO DO, TEXT ON SCREEN: "Ask: How many cases are you carrying?"**
"Here's what you do. Ask them directly: how many active cases are you currently handling? How much time have you been able to spend on mine? The answers won't make you happy. But they'll tell you what you're working with."

**[0:45-0:54] CTA, TEXT ON SCREEN: "imnotanattorney.com"**
"You deserve to know. Know What They Know. Free resources, link in bio."
