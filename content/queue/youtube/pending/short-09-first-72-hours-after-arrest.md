### Full Script

**[0:00-0:04] HOOK — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Just arrested? Do these 5 things NOW."**
[Direct to camera, urgent but steady]
"You just got arrested — or someone you love did. The next 72 hours are critical. Here are five things to do right now."

**[0:04-0:13] RULE 1 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#1: SAY NOTHING"**
"Number one — and this is the most important thing you'll hear today — say nothing. Not to the officers. Not to anyone in holding. Not to your cellmate. The only words you need are: 'I want an attorney before I answer any questions.' Officers are trained to keep conversations going after Miranda. Friendly conversation feels different from interrogation. It isn't."

**[0:13-0:22] RULE 2 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#2: Get an attorney immediately"**
"Number two. Get an attorney. If you can afford one, call them now. If you can't, request a public defender at your first appearance. Do not wait. Do not try to handle this yourself. Do not represent yourself because you think you can explain what happened."

**[0:22-0:30] RULE 3 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#3: Document everything you remember"**
"Number three. Write down everything you remember about the arrest. Where were you? What time? What did officers say? Did they read you your rights? Was there a search? What was the basis? Your memory is sharpest right now. In three months, details fade. Write it down today."

**[0:30-0:38] RULE 4 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#4: Fight for bail reduction"**
"Number four. At your first hearing, fight for bail reduction. First-time offender? Community ties? Employment? Family responsibilities? These all matter. Ask your attorney to argue for lower bail or release on your own recognizance."

**[0:38-0:48] RULE 5 — TEXT ON SCREEN: "#5: Ask your attorney these 3 questions"**
"Number five. As soon as you have an attorney, ask three questions: What are the exact charges and maximum penalties? When will you receive discovery? And when should I expect to hear from you next? Set expectations now. Not after months of silence."

**[0:48-0:58] CTA — TEXT ON SCREEN: "imnotanattorney.com"**
"The first 72 hours set the tone for your entire case. Don't waste them. We research. You ask. Free guides — link in bio."
