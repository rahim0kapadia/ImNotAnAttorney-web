### Full Script

**[0:00-0:05] HOOK — TEXT ON SCREEN: "It's been 8 months. Nothing is happening."**
[Direct to camera, measured frustration]
"Your case has been open for months. Every court date ends with another continuance. Your attorney says 'these things take time.' But there's a critical difference between a case taking long because your attorney is working — and a case taking long because nobody is doing anything."

**[0:05-0:13] WHEN IT'S NORMAL — TEXT ON SCREEN: "Legitimate delay:"**
"Some delays are legitimate. Discovery is massive and takes time to review. Your attorney filed a suppression motion and the court is scheduling the hearing. A co-defendant's situation is developing. Your attorney is running the clock because time is on your side."

**[0:13-0:18] THE TEST — TEXT ON SCREEN: "The question that tells you everything:"**
"Here's how you tell the difference. One question."

**[0:18-0:28] THE QUESTION — TEXT ON SCREEN: "What specific work has been done since our last court date?"**
"Ask your attorney: 'What specific work has been done on my case since our last court date?' A real answer sounds like: 'We filed a motion to suppress on March 15th. The prosecution's response is due April 28th. The hearing is set for May 12th.' That's progress."

**[0:28-0:35] THE BAD ANSWER — TEXT ON SCREEN: "'These things take time' is NOT an answer"**
"A vague answer sounds like: 'We're waiting to hear back.' From whom? About what? 'These things take time' is not an answer. 'We're working on it' is not an answer."

**[0:35-0:45] RED FLAGS — TEXT ON SCREEN: "RED FLAGS:"**
"Red flags: five continuances in a row with no explanation. Zero motions on the court docket after six months. You're the only one initiating contact. The prosecution says they're ready but your side keeps asking for more time."

**[0:45-0:55] CTA — TEXT ON SCREEN: "imnotanattorney.com"**
"The waiting is its own punishment. You can't plan. You can't move. Every decision gets filtered through 'but what about my case.' You deserve a timeline. Know What They Know. Link in bio."
