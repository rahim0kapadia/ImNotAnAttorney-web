## 6. REELS STRATEGY (from Brock Johnson framework)

**Optimal length:** 30-60 seconds for this niche. Legal content needs enough time to establish credibility but not so much that crisis buyers scroll past.

**Hook formula:** Specific number + specific fact + "here's what you do." Examples:
- "Day 7. No callback. Here's what you do."
- "I paid $20,000 to a private attorney. He never filed a single motion."

**Volume from zero:** 2 Reels/week minimum. Reels get pushed to Explore more than carousels for new accounts. After 1,000 followers, can reduce to 1/week.

**Production style:** Raw. No studio. No ring light aesthetic. Plain wall, direct to camera, speaking like a conversation. The lack of production value IS the brand -- it signals "this is a real person who went through this, not a legal content factory."

## 7. GROWTH MECHANICS (first 30 days)

**Week 1-2: Seed the account**
- Post all 5 seed posts
- Follow 50-100 accounts in the criminal defense / DUI space (attorneys, legal news, defendant advocacy)
- Comment genuinely on 10 posts/day in related accounts (not spam -- actual value comments)
- Cross-post Reels to TikTok and Facebook for algorithm signal

**Week 3-4: Comment-to-DM loop**
- ManyChat comment automation live on all posts
- Every post ends with a keyword CTA
- DM sequences convert comments into checklist downloads and Playbook sales
- Track: comment-to-DM conversion rate, DM-to-click rate, click-to-purchase rate

**Milestone targets:**
- Week 1: 5 posts live, ManyChat connected, bio optimized
- Week 2: 50+ followers, 10+ DM conversations
- Week 4: 200+ followers, 50+ DM conversations, first Playbook sale from IG
