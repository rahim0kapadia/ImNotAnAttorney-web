### Slide 2 -- QUESTION 1

**Headline**: Have you subpoenaed the breathalyzer calibration records?

**Body**: Breathalyzers must be calibrated on a strict schedule. If the calibration lapsed or the records don't exist, that number they're using against you might be inadmissible. Your attorney should have already checked.

**Design**: Large amber "1" top-left. "inadmissible" in amber.

**Alt text**: Question 1: Have you subpoenaed the breathalyzer calibration records? Explanation of why calibration lapses can make evidence inadmissible.

---

### Slide 3 -- QUESTION 2

**Headline**: Was the field sobriety test done per NHTSA standards?

**Body**: Flat surface. Well-lit area. Specific instructions for each test. Officers skip steps constantly. If it wasn't administered correctly, the results don't mean what the prosecution says they mean.

**Design**: Large amber "2" top-left. "Officers skip steps constantly" in amber.

**Alt text**: Question 2: Was the field sobriety test administered per NHTSA standards? Explanation of how improper administration invalidates results.

---

### Slide 4 -- QUESTION 3

**Headline**: Does the dashcam support the reason for the stop?

**Body**: The officer wrote a reason for pulling you over. The dashcam either confirms it or it doesn't. If there's no dashcam, ask why. If there is, ask if your attorney has watched it.

**Design**: Large amber "3" top-left. "ask why" in amber.

**Alt text**: Question 3: Does the dashcam footage support the stated reason for the traffic stop?

---

### Slide 5 -- QUESTION 4

**Headline**: What motions have you FILED? Not planned. Filed.

**Body**: Motions to suppress evidence. Motions challenging the stop. Motions on the blood draw procedure. If zero motions have been filed and your attorney can't explain exactly why each one doesn't apply, they haven't done the work.

**Design**: Large amber "4" top-left. "FILED" amber and all-caps. "zero motions" amber in body.

**Alt text**: Question 4: What motions have you actually filed? Not planned, filed.

---

### Slide 6 -- QUESTION 5

**Headline**: If this were YOUR case, would you take this deal?

**Body**: This question cuts through everything. Watch their face when you ask it. A confident attorney answers directly. A lazy one deflects. The answer tells you whether they've actually evaluated your case or they're clearing their calendar.

**Design**: Large amber "5" top-left. "Watch their face" in amber.

**Alt text**: Question 5: If this were your case, would you take this deal?

### Slide 9 -- WHAT TO DO WITH THE ANSWERS

**Headline**: After you ask:

**Body**:
1. Write down every answer -- exact words, not paraphrases
2. Follow up by email so there's a paper trail
3. If they can't answer or won't answer, that IS the answer
4. Compare what they say to what's actually on the court docket

**Design**: Numbers in amber. "that IS the answer" amber, Lato 700.
