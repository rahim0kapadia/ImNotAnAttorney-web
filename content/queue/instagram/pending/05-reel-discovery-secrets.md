### Caption

Your attorney has your discovery. Have you read it?

Discovery is every piece of evidence the prosecution has against you. Police reports. Lab results. Witness statements. Body camera footage. Chain of custody documents. All of it.

You have the legal right to see it. All of it.

Most defendants never ask. They assume their attorney is reviewing it thoroughly. Maybe they are. But here's what I learned when I read mine: I found things my attorney missed. Because I was there. I knew what actually happened. And the documents didn't always match.

A wrong date. A weight discrepancy. An officer statement that contradicted a witness. These details matter. They're the difference between a conviction and a dismissal.

Three questions to ask your attorney this week:
1. Have we received all discovery?
2. Is anything missing that should have been turned over?
3. Can I have a copy to review myself?

Write down the answers. Follow up by email.
