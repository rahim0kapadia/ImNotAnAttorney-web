## Pinning Schedule

**Daily volume**: 8-12 pins per day
**Split**: 70% fresh own content / 30% curated from quality legal education accounts
**Best times for legal/defendant audience**:
- 8:00-9:00 PM EST (evening research, people search legal questions after work)
- 6:00-7:00 AM EST (morning research, people search before court dates)
- 12:00-1:00 PM EST (lunch break searches)

**Seasonal Calendar**:
- January: "New Year, new case strategy", resolution-adjacent content about taking control of your defense
- April: Tax season, white collar and federal content push
- June-August: DUI content ramp (summer = more DUI arrests). Pin DUI content 30-45 days ahead starting mid-May.
- September: Back-to-school, drug possession content (college-age defendants)
- November-December: Holiday DUI content pinned by mid-October
- Year-round: Attorney accountability and court prep content (no seasonality)

**Content Rotation**:
- Week 1-2: Create 3-5 fresh pin designs for 5 blog posts (15-25 new pins)
- Week 3-4: Create 3-5 fresh pin designs for next 5 blog posts
- Ongoing: Rotate through all 35 blog posts creating fresh designs every 8-10 weeks
- Goal: Every blog post has 5+ unique pin designs within first 3 months
