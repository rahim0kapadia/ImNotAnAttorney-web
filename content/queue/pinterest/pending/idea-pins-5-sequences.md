## Appendix: Idea Pin Publishing Notes

### Scheduling
- Publish 1 Idea Pin per week
- Best days: Tuesday through Thursday (legal searches peak mid-week when people are preparing for upcoming court dates)
- Best time: 7:00-9:00 PM EST (evening research hours)
- Suggested order: Pin 5 (Arraignment) first — broadest appeal, then Pin 2 (Traffic Stop), then Pin 1 (Questions), Pin 4 (Attorney Signs), Pin 3 (Discovery)

### Tagging
- Tag each Idea Pin with relevant topic tags in Pinterest's creator tools
- Add product tags where applicable (link to Defense Playbooks when Pinterest enables Idea Pin shopping)
- Tag brand collaborators if any (none currently)

### Repurposing
- Each Idea Pin slide can be repurposed as an individual Standard Pin with a link to the corresponding blog post
- This means 5 Idea Pins generate 25+ individual pin images for the Standard Pin content calendar
- Slide designs should work both in sequence AND as standalone images

### Metrics to Track
- Save rate per Idea Pin (benchmark: 2-5% for educational content)
- Follower growth attributable to Idea Pins
- Slide-through rate (what percentage reach the final CTA slide)
- Which specific slides get the most close-ups (indicates screenshot/save behavior)
