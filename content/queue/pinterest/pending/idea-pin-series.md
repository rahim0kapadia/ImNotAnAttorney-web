## Appendix: Idea Pin Publishing Notes

### Scheduling
- Publish 1 Idea Pin per week
- Best days: Tuesday through Thursday (legal searches peak mid-week when people prepare for upcoming court dates)
- Best time: 7:00-9:00 PM EST (evening research hours)
- Suggested publishing order:
  1. Series 2 (DUI Timeline) -- broadest appeal, most urgent search intent
  2. Series 3 (Attorney Accountability) -- INNA's core brand territory, high emotional resonance
  3. Series 1 (10 Questions) -- comprehensive authority builder, positions account as the definitive question resource

### Tagging
- Tag each Idea Pin with relevant topic tags in Pinterest's creator tools
- Add product tags where applicable (link to Defense Milestone Score quiz when Pinterest enables Idea Pin shopping)
- Tag brand collaborators if any (none currently)

### Repurposing
- Each Idea Pin slide can be repurposed as an individual Standard Pin with a link to the corresponding blog post
- Series 1 (10 Questions) = 12 slides = 10 potential standard pins (one per question slide)
- Series 2 (DUI Timeline) = 8 slides = 6 potential standard pins (excluding hook and CTA)
- Series 3 (Attorney Signs) = 9 slides = 7 potential standard pins (one per sign slide)
- Total: 23 individual pin images from 3 Idea Pin series
- Slide designs should work both in sequence AND as standalone images

### Metrics to Track
- Save rate per Idea Pin (benchmark: 2-5% for educational content)

- Slide-through rate (what percentage of viewers reach the final CTA slide)
