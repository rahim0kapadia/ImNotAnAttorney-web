DUI cases get dismissed every day.

Not because of lucky technicalities, because of real, specific errors that officers make during the stop, the field tests, and chemical testing.

Here are the defenses your attorney should be investigating right now:
===TWEET===
Defense 1: The traffic stop was illegal

Officers need REASONABLE SUSPICION to pull you over. That means a specific, articulable reason. "Weaving within your lane" is frequently challenged and thrown out. "Driving slowly" is almost never sufficient.
===TWEET===
If the stop was illegal, everything that followed, field tests, breathalyzer, arrest, can be suppressed.
===TWEET===
Defense 2: Field sobriety tests were done incorrectly

Standardized FSTs have strict protocols from NHTSA.
===TWEET===
Common officer errors:
• Wrong surface (uneven/sloped)
• Didn't ask about medical conditions affecting balance
• Wrong scoring
• Non-standardized tests with no scientific validation

Your attorney should compare the officer's certification records against what the footage shows.
===TWEET===
Defense 3: Breathalyzer results are unreliable

Issues that make BAC readings challengeable:
• Not calibrated within the required schedule
• Operator not properly certified
• Observation period skipped or shortened
• Recent mouthwash/acid reflux inflating readings
• Breathalyzer…
===TWEET===
005–0. 02 margin of error

If you blew . 08 or . 09, that margin alone might put you under.
===TWEET===
Defense 4: Rising blood alcohol

Alcohol takes 30-90 minutes to fully absorb. If you had your last drink shortly before driving, your BAC may have been below . 08 while you were actually driving, and only rose above . 08 by the time you were tested.
===TWEET===
This is a legitimate defense that requires expert testimony.
