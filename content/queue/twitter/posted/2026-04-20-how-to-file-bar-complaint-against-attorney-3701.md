Your attorney has stopped communicating. Missed a deadline. Lied to you about your case.

You're thinking about filing a bar complaint.

Here's what that process actually is — and what it can and can't do for you:
===TWEET===
What the bar CAN do:
✓ Investigate your complaint
✓ Require the attorney to respond in writing
✓ Issue reprimands, suspend or revoke their license

What the bar CANNOT do:
✗ Get your money back
✗ Change the outcome of your case
✗ Force your attorney to do specific work

Here's the useful part most people miss:

The moment your attorney receives a bar complaint, their behavior often changes dramatically. Not because they suddenly care — because they're now worried about their license.
===TWEET===
The notification alone sometimes creates accountability that months of ignored calls couldn't.
===TWEET===
Clear grounds for a complaint:
✓ Abandonment — stopped communicating, not working your case
✓ Missing deadlines that harmed your case
✓ Lying to you about what's been filed or the status of your case
✓ Commingling your retainer funds with their personal money
