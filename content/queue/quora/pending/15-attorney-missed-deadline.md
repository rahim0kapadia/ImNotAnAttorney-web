## Quora: INNA-Q15 | My lawyer missed a filing deadline. What are my options?
**Topics**: Attorney Malpractice, Missed Deadlines, Criminal Defense, Defendant Rights, Legal Ethics
**SEO**: "attorney missed deadline" / "lawyer missed filing" / "criminal defense malpractice deadline"

Deadlines in criminal cases are not suggestions. They're hard lines. Miss a filing deadline and you might lose the right to file that motion forever. Miss an appellate notice and your appeal could be dead on arrival.

**Common deadlines your attorney should be tracking:**
- Suppression motions: often 30-45 days after arraignment
- Discovery demands: early in the case
- Plea deadlines: date to accept or reject an offer
- Appellate notices: 30 days from sentencing (this one is absolute)
- DMV hearings: 7-15 days in most states

**The consequences vary by severity:**

Severe: Missed appellate notice means you generally cannot appeal. Missed mandatory motion deadline means you lose that right forever.

Serious: Missed suppression motion deadline means evidence that could have been excluded becomes admissible. That's potentially case-changing.

Moderate: Some deadlines have grace periods or late filing provisions.

**What to do right now:**

Document what happened. What deadline was missed? When did you learn about it? What was the consequence? Send an email: "I'm writing to confirm that [specific deadline] was missed on [date]. Please advise on the consequences and what can be done."

Your attorney's response tells you everything. Good responses: "I acknowledge the mistake. Here's what we can still do." Bad responses: "Don't worry about it." Or silence.

**Your options depending on severity:**

Motion to file late -- some deadlines have "good cause" provisions. Your attorney should know if this applies.

Change attorneys -- if this is part of a pattern of neglect, this may be the final straw.

Bar complaint -- if the missed deadline caused actual harm, document everything and file with your state bar.

Legal malpractice -- requires proving duty (they owed you care), breach (missing deadlines qualifies), causation (the breach caused harm), and damages (quantifiable harm). This is hard to prove but it's real.

One missed deadline might be an honest mistake. A pattern of missed deadlines is negligence. Ask yourself: How many times has this happened? Does my attorney seem organized? Do I have confidence in them going forward?
