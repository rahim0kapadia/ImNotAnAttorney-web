## Quora: INNA-Q18 | How do I file a bar complaint against my criminal defense attorney?
**Topics**: Bar Complaint, Attorney Discipline, Legal Ethics, Attorney Malpractice, Defendant Rights
**SEO**: "how to file bar complaint" / "bar complaint against attorney" / "state bar complaint process"

Before you file, know what a bar complaint actually is, and what it isn't.

**What the bar CAN do:** Investigate your complaint. Require the attorney to respond. Issue a reprimand (private or public). Suspend the attorney's license. Disbar them. Order ethics courses.

**What the bar CANNOT do:** Get your money back. Change the outcome of your case. Award you damages. Force your attorney to do specific work.

A bar complaint is about professional discipline, not about fixing your individual case.

**Clear grounds for a complaint:**
- Abandonment, stopped communicating entirely, no work being done
- Missed deadlines that harmed your case, a filing deadline passed and your rights were lost
- Lying to you, told you they filed something they didn't
- Commingling funds, your retainer money used for personal expenses
- Conflicts of interest, representing someone with opposing interests

**Weaker grounds (but worth documenting):** Slow to return calls but eventually does. Strategy disagreements. Bad outcome where the attorney did competent work. Personality conflicts.

**How to file:**

Step 1: Find your state bar's complaint process. Search "[your state] bar complaint." Every state has an online or downloadable form.

Step 2: Gather documentation, retainer agreement, all communication records, court docket printout showing what has and hasn't been filed, a timeline of events, evidence of specific harm.

Step 3: Write a clear, factual complaint. The ones that get attention are specific ("missed the suppression motion deadline on March 15"), factual (what happened, not how you feel), documented (attach evidence for every claim), and organized chronologically.

Step 4: Submit and wait. The bar will review, send it to the attorney for response (they MUST respond), investigate if warranted, and make a determination. Timeline: 6-12+ months. This is not a quick fix.

**The practical effect:** Many attorneys who receive a bar complaint suddenly become very responsive. Not because they care more, because they're now worried about their license. Even dismissed complaints create a record, force a written response, and often improve behavior.

**Important advice: Switch first. File later.** Your immediate priority is your criminal case. Get a competent attorney working on it. Then file the bar complaint once your case is stabilized. Filing while your case is still active with the same attorney can create an adversarial dynamic.
