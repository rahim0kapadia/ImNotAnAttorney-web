## Quora: INNA-Q25 | I was charged with drug trafficking but I've never sold anything. How is that possible?
**Topics**: Drug Charges, Drug Trafficking, Constructive Possession, Mandatory Minimums, Criminal Defense
**SEO**: "drug trafficking charge just a user" / "constructive possession defense" / "trafficking charge weight threshold"

Because trafficking charges in most states aren't based on what you did. They're based on what they weighed.

In most jurisdictions, drug trafficking isn't defined by evidence of selling. It's defined by weight thresholds. If the substance exceeds a certain weight, the charge automatically escalates from possession to trafficking, regardless of intent, regardless of whether you've ever sold a single thing to anyone.

A person who buys a personal supply that crosses the weight threshold gets the same charge as someone running a distribution operation. The law doesn't distinguish between the two. The scale does.

And mandatory minimum sentences are tied to those weight thresholds. Once it's trafficking, even a sympathetic judge may be legally required to impose a minimum prison sentence.

**Where your defense may live: constructive possession.**

If the drugs weren't physically on your person, the prosecution must prove constructive possession, which requires two things:

1. Knowledge, you knew the substance was there
2. Dominion and control, you had the ability to exercise control over it

Both elements must be proven beyond a reasonable doubt. If drugs were found in a car with three passengers, the prosecution can't just point at whoever was sitting closest. They have to prove YOU knew they were there and YOU had control over them.

**The weight problem your attorney should be scrutinizing:**

The weight used to determine your charge might not be the weight of the actual drug. In many cases, law enforcement weighs the entire substance including cutting agents, fillers, residue, and packaging material. That means 2 grams of actual drug mixed with 18 grams of baking soda gets charged as 20 grams. In documented cases, there have been 73% weight discrepancies between scene measurements and lab confirmation. That's the difference between possession and trafficking. Between probation and a mandatory prison sentence.

**Seven questions to ask your attorney:**
1. What evidence does the State have that I had dominion and control over the substance?
2. Has a constructive possession defense been considered?
3. What was the actual weight at the lab vs. the weight reported at the scene?
4. Does the weight include cutting agents, packaging, or non-drug material?
5. What is the mandatory minimum I'm facing, and is there any way to get below it?
6. Has anyone else been charged in connection with this case?
7. What motions can we file to challenge the weight or the search?

If your attorney isn't challenging the weight, they're accepting the prosecution's number, and that number determines your mandatory minimum.
