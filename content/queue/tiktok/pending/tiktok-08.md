# TikTok Script #8: Ask Your DUI Attorney About the 15-Minute Observation Period

**Topic:** Observation period requirement before breathalyzer test
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + demonstration
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + stopwatch tick ambient

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A stopwatch showing 15:00 counting down. Camera slowly pulls back to reveal an empty patrol car seat -- nobody is watching.
**Text overlay**: "The officer was supposed to watch you for 15 minutes before the test."
**Audio**: Stopwatch ticking. Eerily quiet.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, knowing look.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Did they? Because if they didn't, your breathalyzer result might not mean anything."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Split screen -- left side shows what SHOULD happen (officer watching defendant for 15 min), right shows what ACTUALLY happens (officer filling out paperwork, on radio, turned away).
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Before a breath test, the officer is supposed to observe you continuously for 15 minutes. No eating. No drinking. No burping. No vomiting. Because any of those things can contaminate the sample and spike the reading. Residual mouth alcohol from a burp alone can push a .05 to a .12."
**Text overlay**: "15 MIN OBSERVATION. Burp = false high reading. Were they watching?"

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Observation period log document -- with blank fields highlighted.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "There's a log for this. The observation period log is supposed to document that the officer watched you for the full 15 minutes. Ask your attorney: does the log exist? Is it complete? Does the timeline even make sense -- was there physically enough time between the stop and the test?"
**Text overlay**: "ASK: Does the observation period log exist? Is it complete?"

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Timeline showing arrest time, observation start, and test time -- with the math not adding up. Red circle around the gap.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "In some cases, the math simply doesn't work. The stop was at 11:42. The test was at 11:51. Nine minutes. Where's the other six?"
**Text overlay**: "Stop: 11:42 PM. Test: 11:51 PM. That's 9 minutes, not 15."

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Stopwatch from the opening -- now stopped at 9:00 with "INCOMPLETE" stamped across it.
**Text overlay**: "If they didn't follow the rules, the result isn't science. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Stopwatch stops. Clean beat.
