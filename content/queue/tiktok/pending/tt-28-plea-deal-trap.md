## TikTok Script: The Plea Deal Trap

**Format**: Standard
**Duration**: 45 seconds
**Suggested sound**: Original audio, voiceover + suspense beat
**Suggested posting time**: Thursday 8:00 PM EST

---

[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A plea agreement document on a table. Pen hovering over the signature line. Hand trembling slightly.
**Text overlay**: "'Sign by Friday or the offer gets worse.'"
**Audio**: Clock ticking. Pen tapping.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Pen gets set down. Person pushes the document away slightly.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "That line, 'sign by Friday or it gets worse', is a negotiation tactic. Not a fact. Let me explain the trap."

[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Diagram showing the plea timeline.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The plea deal trap works like this. Your attorney gets the first offer early, before reviewing all discovery, before filing motions, before doing any real defense work. Then they present it as urgent. 'Take it before they pull it.' But here's what they're not telling you: the first offer is almost always the worst one."
**Text overlay**: "First offer = worst offer. Almost always."

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: Graph showing plea offers over time vs. defense activity.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "As your attorney files motions, as discovery reveals weaknesses, as the prosecution realizes they might actually have to try this case, the offers get better. Prosecutors don't want trials. Trials cost them time and resources. An attorney who's visibly preparing for trial gets better deals than one who's visibly ready to settle."
**Text overlay**: "More defense work = better offers. Trial prep = leverage."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: Cut to person, more intimate shot.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The collateral consequences are the real trap though. When you plead guilty, you're not just accepting a sentence. You're accepting: a criminal record. Potential job loss. Housing discrimination. Custody implications. Immigration consequences. Loss of gun rights. Loss of voting rights in some states. These follow you for LIFE. And most attorneys don't walk you through all of them."
**Text overlay**: "Collateral consequences: job, housing, custody, immigration, voting, FOR LIFE"

[SECOND 32-40]
**Visual**: Text list of questions on screen.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Before you sign anything: Has all discovery been received? What motions have been filed? What are the specific collateral consequences? Can we counter-offer? And, is this offer likely to improve if we continue building the defense?"
**Text overlay**: Questions appearing one at a time.

[SECOND 40-45]
**Visual**: Plea agreement from the start, but now with notes written on it, questions in the margins, "NOT YET" written next to the signature line.
**Text overlay**: "Don't sign in the dark. Know What They Know. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Clock stops. Beat resolves.
