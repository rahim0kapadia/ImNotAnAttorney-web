[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Clock on a wall. Close-up of the second hand ticking. Time reads "HOUR 1."
**Text overlay**: "You just got arrested. The clock is running."
**Audio**: Clock ticking. Heartbeat.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to person, urgent but calm. Like a coach in the locker room at halftime.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The first 72 hours after arrest matter more than the next 72 weeks. Here's your survival guide."

[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: "HOUR 0-6" banner. Timeline building.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Hours zero through six, you're being booked. The only thing you say to anyone is: 'I want an attorney before I answer any questions.' Not 'I didn't do it.' Not 'Let me explain.' Not 'Is this about the thing at, ' NOTHING. Do not talk to police. Do not talk to other inmates. Do not post on social media. Everything is evidence."
**Text overlay**: "HOURS 0-6: Say ONE thing. 'I want an attorney.' Then STOP."

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: "HOUR 6-24" banner.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Hours six through twenty-four, you'll see a judge for a bond hearing. If you have family or friends, they should be gathering information for your bail argument: your employment, your ties to the community, your lack of criminal history. A good bail argument can get you home. A bad one keeps you in a cell during your entire case."
**Text overlay**: "HOURS 6-24: Bond hearing. Employment, community ties, clean record = your argument."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: "HOUR 24-48" banner.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Hours twenty-four through forty-eight, if you're out on bail, start organizing. Get a notebook. Write down everything you remember about what happened, every detail, every person present, every conversation. Your memory is sharpest now. In three months, details fade. Write it NOW."
**Text overlay**: "HOURS 24-48: WRITE EVERYTHING DOWN. Memory fades. Details save cases."

[SECOND 32-42]
**Visual**: "HOUR 48-72" banner. Person at desk, writing and organizing.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Hours forty-eight through seventy-two, evaluate your attorney. If you have a public defender, find out their caseload. If you're hiring private, use the consultation as a two-way evaluation. Ask: who specifically handles my case? What is your trial record? How many active cases do you carry? Do not sign a retainer without answers."
**Text overlay**: "HOURS 48-72: Evaluate your attorney. Ask the hard questions before you pay."

[SECOND 42-50]
**Visual**: Checklist appearing on screen, one item at a time. Each gets checked off.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Your 72-hour checklist. One, invoke your right to counsel. Two, do not talk to anyone about your case. Three, fight for bail. Four, write down every detail you remember. Five, evaluate your attorney before committing. Six, request discovery as soon as possible."
**Text overlay**: Checklist items checking off.

[SECOND 50-57]
**Visual**: Clock from the opening, but now showing "HOUR 72" with a completed checklist next to it.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The defendants who do well in the system aren't the ones who panic. They're the ones who organize. The first 72 hours set the tone for your entire case."
**Text overlay**: "First 72 hours set the tone for everything."

[SECOND 57-60]
**Visual**: Notebook from earlier, full of notes, organized. Direct eye contact.
**Text overlay**: "Don't panic. Organize. Know What They Know. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Clock stops. Heartbeat fades. Confident beat resolves.
