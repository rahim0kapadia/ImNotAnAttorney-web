**Topic:** Weight discrepancy in drug trafficking case
**Format:** storytime + text overlay + dramatic reveal

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A digital scale showing "93.9g" on screen. Then it flickers and changes to "25.59g"
**Text overlay**: "68 grams disappeared. Nobody asked where they went."
**Audio**: Scale beeping. Static crackle.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, leaning forward, intense but measured.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "A defendant was facing trafficking charges with a mandatory minimum. Then he opened his own discovery."

## Body
[SECOND 3-15]
**Visual**: Stack of papers -- 500 pages visible. Person flipping through them at a table late at night.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "He'd paid his attorney thousands. No motions filed. Calls weren't returned. One night, he decided to read his own 500-page discovery file. No legal training. Just a defendant who was tired of being in the dark. Within a week, he found four problems his attorney had never mentioned."
**Text overlay**: "500 pages. Zero legal training. 4 problems the attorney missed."

[SECOND 15-25]
**Visual**: Scale graphic returns. "93.9g at scene" on left. "25.59g at lab" on right. Red arrow between them: "73% DISCREPANCY"
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Problem one: the officers weighed the substance at 93.9 grams at the scene. The lab weighed it at 25.59 grams. That's 68 grams missing -- a 73% discrepancy. In a trafficking case, weight determines everything. It's the difference between a possession charge with probation and a trafficking charge with mandatory prison. His attorney had never mentioned it."
**Text overlay**: "73% WEIGHT DISCREPANCY. Attorney said nothing."

[SECOND 25-35]
**Visual**: Phone screen showing two phone numbers -- one labeled "Confidential Informant" and one labeled "Defendant." They're the same number.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Problem two: a phone number in the discovery was attributed to both the confidential informant AND the defendant. Same number. Two different people. That's either sloppy police work or something worse. His attorney hadn't flagged it."
**Text overlay**: "Same phone number. Two different people. Attorney missed it."

[SECOND 35-42]
**Visual**: Lab report showing "MDMA/MDA" highlighted, next to a charging document showing "amphetamine."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Problem three: the charges said amphetamine. The lab said MDMA and MDA. Different drug. Different scheduling. Different sentence. His attorney had filed nothing."
**Text overlay**: "Charged with amphetamine. Lab says MDMA. Different crime entirely."

[SECOND 42-48]
**Visual**: Fingerprint card showing "21 latent prints recovered." Next to it: "0 matches to defendant."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Problem four: 21 latent fingerprints were recovered from the evidence. Zero matched the defendant. Twenty-one prints. None of them his. Not a single motion filed on any of this."
**Text overlay**: "21 fingerprints. ZERO matched the defendant."

[SECOND 48-55]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, calm but firm.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "He found all four of these issues himself. With no legal degree. Just by reading his own paperwork. You have the right to see every piece of evidence the prosecution has against you. The question is: has your attorney actually looked at it?"
**Text overlay**: "You have the right to read your own discovery."

**Visual**: The scale from the opening. This time it reads: "YOUR QUESTIONS MATTER."
