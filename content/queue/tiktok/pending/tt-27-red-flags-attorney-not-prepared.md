## TikTok Script: Red Flags Your Attorney Isn't Prepared

**Format**: Standard
**Duration**: 30 seconds
**Suggested sound**: Original audio, voiceover + alert beat
**Suggested posting time**: Friday 6:00 PM EST

---

[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Courtroom hallway. Attorney flipping through a file, clearly reading it for the first time. Close-up of the frantic page-turning.
**Text overlay**: "5 minutes before your hearing. Your attorney is reading your file for the first time."
**Audio**: Rapid page flipping.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to person, eyebrows raised.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If you've seen this, you already know. Here are the red flags."

[SECOND 3-8]
**Visual**: Red flag cards appearing rapidly, each with an alarm icon.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "They confuse your charges with another client's. They ask YOU questions they should already know the answers to. They can't name the prosecutor or the judge without checking."
**Text overlay**:
- "Confuses your charges"
- "Asks you basic questions about your own case"
- "Doesn't know the judge or prosecutor"

[SECOND 8-15]
**Visual**: More red flag cards.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "They give you a hallway update instead of a real meeting. They use the phrase 'let me look into that' for the third time without ever looking into it. They ask for another continuance without explaining what they'll do with the time."
**Text overlay**:
- "Hallway updates only"
- "'Let me look into that', again"
- "Continuance with no plan"

[SECOND 15-22]
**Visual**: Cut to person, practical tone.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "What to do right now. Before your next court date, email your attorney: 'Can we schedule a meeting to prepare? I have specific questions about motions, discovery, and strategy.' Their response, or lack of response, tells you everything you need to know."
**Text overlay**: "Email before court: 'Can we meet to prepare?' Watch the response."

[SECOND 22-27]
**Visual**: Phone showing a sent email. Timestamp. Waiting for reply.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "An unprepared attorney doesn't suddenly become prepared because your court date arrived. Preparation happens in the weeks before, or it doesn't happen at all."
**Text overlay**: "Preparation happens before court day. Or not at all."

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Courtroom hallway again, but now the person is the one holding the file, organized, with tabs and notes.
**Text overlay**: "If they won't prepare, YOU prepare. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Beat resolves.
