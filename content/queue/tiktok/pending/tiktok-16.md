# TikTok Script #16: The Motion Your Attorney Should Have Filed 30 Days Ago

**Topic:** Suppression motion deadlines that close permanently
**Format:** talking head + calendar visual + urgency
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio, voiceover + ticking tension ambient

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A calendar with a date circled in red. The word "DEADLINE" above it. A big red X stamps over it: "MISSED."
**Text overlay**: "This motion deadline closed 30 days ago. Your attorney never filed."
**Audio**: Stamp sound. Heavy silence.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, controlled intensity.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Some motion deadlines, once missed, close forever. The right to challenge that evidence? Gone. Permanently."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Timeline showing arraignment date, then a window labeled "SUPPRESSION MOTION WINDOW: 30-45 DAYS" that closes with a lock icon.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Suppression motions, the ones that ask the judge to throw out illegally obtained evidence, often have to be filed within 30 to 45 days of arraignment. If your attorney misses that window, you permanently lose the right to challenge how the evidence was obtained. Doesn't matter if the search was illegal. Doesn't matter if the stop had no basis. The window closed. You're stuck with the evidence."
**Text overlay**: "SUPPRESSION MOTION: 30-45 days after arraignment. Miss it = waived FOREVER."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Two case paths side by side. "MOTION FILED: Evidence suppressed. Case dismissed." vs. "DEADLINE MISSED: Evidence stays. Mandatory minimum."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "In drug cases, suppressing the drugs can get the entire case dismissed. In DUI cases, suppressing the traffic stop eliminates everything that followed. These motions can end your case before trial. But only if they're filed on time."
**Text overlay**: "Suppression can END the case. But only if filed on time."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Text list of questions.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your attorney right now: what is the deadline for filing a suppression motion in my case? Has it already passed? If not, are you planning to file one? And what specific grounds would it be based on?"
**Text overlay**: "ASK: What is the suppression deadline? Has it passed? Are you filing one?"

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Calendar from the opening, but rewound to before the deadline, with "MOTION TO SUPPRESS: FILED" written in green.
**Text overlay**: "Don't let deadlines pass in silence. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Lock clicks open. Confident beat.

## CTA
If your case is active, ask this question TODAY. Follow for more.
