# TikTok Script #3: The Breathalyzer That Convicted You Might Be Lying

**Topic:** Breathalyzer calibration records and margin of error
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + visual demonstration
**Duration:** 45s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + dark tension beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A breathalyzer device on screen. A digital reading flickers between .07, .08, .09 -- unstable, uncertain.
**Text overlay**: "This machine has a margin of error. Nobody mentioned that."
**Audio**: Electronic glitch sound.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, eyebrow raised.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Your BAC result is not a fact. It's an opinion from a machine."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Infographic showing margin of error: .08 reading could actually be .06 to .10.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Most breath testing devices have a margin of error of plus or minus .005 to .02 BAC. That means a reading of .08 -- the legal limit -- could actually be .06. Legal. Or .10. Way over. The machine doesn't tell you which one. Nobody mentions this at the scene."
**Text overlay**: ".08 reading = could be .06 to .10. Which one is it?"

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: Calendar graphic showing calibration schedule -- some dates crossed out, one circled in red with "OVERDUE."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Breathalyzers are required to be calibrated every 30, 60, or 90 days depending on the model. If the device was overdue for calibration, every reading it produces is questionable. These records exist. They're obtainable. Your attorney should have them."
**Text overlay**: "Calibration overdue? Every reading is questionable."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: Text appearing -- four records to request.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your attorney: have you subpoenaed the calibration records by device serial number? The maintenance and repair logs? The operator certification? And the observation period log -- because the officer is supposed to watch you for 15 minutes before the test to make sure you don't burp, vomit, or put anything in your mouth. Did they?"
**Text overlay**: "4 RECORDS: Calibration. Maintenance. Operator cert. Observation log."

[SECOND 32-40]
**Visual**: Close-up of a FOIA/records request form being filled out.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Barry Scheck -- the attorney who dismantled the forensic evidence in the OJ Simpson case -- built his career on one principle: never trust the conclusion. Audit the process that produced it. That applies directly to your breathalyzer."
**Text overlay**: "Never trust the conclusion. Audit the process."

[SECOND 40-45]
**Visual**: Breathalyzer from the opening -- but now the screen shows "CALIBRATION RECORDS REQUESTED" instead of a BAC number.
**Text overlay**: "The number is only as good as the machine. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Tension resolves. Clean beat.
