## TikTok Script: How to Read Your Own Discovery

**Format**: Standard
**Duration**: 45 seconds
**Suggested sound**: Original audio, investigative voiceover + tense ambient
**Suggested posting time**: Friday 12:00 PM EST

---

[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Close-up of a thick stack of papers being dropped on a desk. Title page visible: "STATE vs. [REDACTED], DISCOVERY MATERIALS"
**Text overlay**: "This is your discovery. Have you read it?"
**Audio**: Heavy paper thud.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Hands flipping through pages. Camera catches police reports, lab results, witness statements.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Most defendants never see their own discovery. Their attorney 'handles it.' That's a mistake."

[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Close-up of a police report. Finger pointing at specific details, times, descriptions, observations.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You were there. You know what happened. Read the police report and compare it to your memory. Does the timeline match? Do the descriptions match? Is the officer saying he saw something that you know he couldn't have seen from where he was standing?"
**Text overlay**: "You were there. You'll catch things your attorney won't."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: New page, witness statement. Red circles being drawn around inconsistencies.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Look at witness statements. Do two witnesses tell the same story? Do their descriptions match each other? Do they match the officer's report? Inconsistencies are ammunition."
**Text overlay**: "Inconsistencies between witnesses = ammunition"

[SECOND 20-30]
**Visual**: Hands counting pages, then flipping to a gap, missing page numbers.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Count the pages. Check the numbers. If page 14 jumps to page 17, something is missing. If body cam footage should exist but isn't included, that's not an oversight. That's information. What's ABSENT from discovery matters as much as what's present."
**Text overlay**: "What's ABSENT matters as much as what's present"

[SECOND 30-38]
**Visual**: Notebook next to discovery. Someone writing notes, "Officer says 11:42 PM, but receipt shows I was at gas station at 11:35."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Make notes. Every inconsistency, every detail that doesn't match your memory, every gap. Bring that list to your attorney. Now THEY have to address it."
**Text overlay**: "Make notes. Bring the list. Change the dynamic."

[SECOND 38-45]
**Visual**: Person closing the discovery folder, placing their hand on top of it. Direct eye contact with camera.
**Text overlay**: "Your case. Your evidence. Your right to read it. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Request your discovery. Read every page. You have that right."

### Caption
Most defendants never read their own discovery. Your attorney "handles it." But you were there. You know what happened. You'll catch things they won't. Request your discovery. Read every page. Make notes on every inconsistency.
