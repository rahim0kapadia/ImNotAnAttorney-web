# TikTok Script #14: Your Trafficking Charge Isn't About What You Did

**Topic:** Weight-based trafficking charges vs. actual intent
**Format:** talking head + scale visual + dramatic reveal
**Duration:** 45s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + heavy bass drops on reveals

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A scale showing "20 grams" on screen. A gavel slams next to it. Text appears: "TRAFFICKING -- MANDATORY PRISON."
**Text overlay**: "You've never sold drugs in your life. But you're facing a trafficking charge."
**Audio**: Gavel slam. Bass drop.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, slow head shake.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Trafficking charges in most states aren't based on what you did. They're based on what they weighed."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Weight threshold infographic -- a sliding scale showing possession on the left, trafficking on the right, with a weight threshold line in the middle.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If the substance found exceeds a certain weight, the charge automatically escalates from possession to trafficking. Regardless of intent. Regardless of whether you've ever sold a single thing to anyone. A person who buys a personal supply that crosses the weight threshold gets the same charge as someone running a distribution operation. The law doesn't distinguish. The scale does."
**Text overlay**: "The scale decides your charge. Not what you actually did."

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: Two containers. One shows "2g ACTUAL DRUG" mixed with "18g BAKING SODA." Total: 20g. The 20g triggers "TRAFFICKING."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "And it gets worse. In many cases, they weigh EVERYTHING -- cutting agents, fillers, residue, packaging material, moisture. Two grams of actual drug mixed with 18 grams of baking soda? You're charged on 20. In one real case, 93.9 grams at the scene became 25.59 at the lab. Seventy-three percent missing. The difference between mandatory prison and probation."
**Text overlay**: "2g drug + 18g filler = charged on 20g. 73% weight discrepancy in real case."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: Legal diagram showing "Constructive Possession" requirements: KNOWLEDGE + CONTROL = both must be proven.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If the drugs weren't physically on you -- if they were found in a car with three passengers or a house with multiple residents -- the prosecution has to prove constructive possession. That means proving two things: you KNEW the drugs were there AND you had the ability to CONTROL them. Both. Beyond reasonable doubt. Being nearby is not enough."
**Text overlay**: "Constructive possession: KNOWLEDGE + CONTROL. Both required. Proximity is not enough."

[SECOND 32-40]
**Visual**: Questions appearing on screen.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your attorney: what is the lab-confirmed weight versus the scene weight? Were cutting agents or packaging included in the total? Has constructive possession been challenged? Were my fingerprints or DNA found on the substance? And -- has a motion to suppress the search itself been considered?"
**Text overlay**: Questions appearing as spoken.

[SECOND 40-45]
**Visual**: The scale from the opening -- but now it reads "QUESTIONS FILED" instead of a weight.
**Text overlay**: "The scale doesn't tell the whole story. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Bass resolves. Clean.
