# TikTok Script #18: The Proffer Trap Nobody Warns You About

**Topic:** Federal cooperation agreements and proffer session risks
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + dark visual tone
**Duration:** 45s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + ominous drone building to reveal

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A conference room. Two chairs on one side (government), one on the other (you). A document on the table: "PROFFER AGREEMENT."
**Text overlay**: "The prosecutor says: 'Cooperate and we'll help you.'"
**Audio**: Door closing. Chair scraping. Heavy atmosphere.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, leaning forward, warning tone.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Those words sound like an offer. They're actually a legal minefield. Let me explain what happens in that room."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Diagram: "WHAT THE PROFFER PROTECTS" (small box) vs. "WHAT IT DOESN'T PROTECT" (much larger box).
**Audio/Voiceover**: "A proffer session -- 'queen for a day' -- is where you tell the government what you know. The proffer agreement says they won't use your words directly against you at trial. That's it. That's all it protects. Everything else is fair game."
**Text overlay**: "Protects: your exact words at trial. That's it. Everything else? Fair game."

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: Text list of what's NOT protected, each appearing with a red X.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Not protected: derivative use. You mention a bank account? They can go find it and use those records against you. Your words are the roadmap. Impeachment -- if you testify differently at trial, they use the proffer to destroy your credibility. Lies void everything -- if the government decides you lied about anything, even something minor, the entire agreement is void and every word you said becomes evidence."
**Text overlay**: "Derivative use: FAIR GAME. Impeachment: FAIR GAME. One lie: EVERYTHING becomes evidence."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: Split path diagram -- "PROFFER GOES WELL" leads to cooperation agreement. "PROFFER GOES WRONG" leads to worse charges + your own admissions used against you.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Here's the scenario that plays out more often than you'd think. You go in trying to be helpful but minimize your own role. You shade things slightly. Leave out a detail. Months later, the government finds the truth through other witnesses. Now your proffer isn't just useless. It's evidence of consciousness of guilt. And lying to federal prosecutors is itself a crime."
**Text overlay**: "Half-truths in a proffer = worse than saying nothing."

[SECOND 32-40]
**Visual**: Questions appearing in clean white text on black.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Before agreeing to proffer, ask your attorney: exactly what does the proffer agreement protect and not protect? What happens if the government decides I wasn't fully truthful? Can my statements be used in civil or regulatory proceedings? And -- is there a strategic advantage to NOT proffering?"
**Text overlay**: Questions appearing as spoken.

[SECOND 40-45]
**Visual**: The conference room from the opening -- but the chair on the defendant's side is now empty. The person walked out.
**Text overlay**: "Know what you're walking into. We Research. You Ask. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Drone resolves. Door closes.

## Caption
"Cooperate and we'll help you at sentencing." Sounds great until you understand what a proffer actually protects -- and what it doesn't. Your words can't be used directly, but everything they lead to CAN. One half-truth voids the entire agreement. Ask your attorney these questions BEFORE you walk into that room.
