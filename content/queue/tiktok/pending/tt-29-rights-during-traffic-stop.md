[SECOND 3-8]
**Visual**: Text cards appearing, each one a right. Clean, easy to read.

- "DO NOT have to answer: 'Where are you coming from?'"

[SECOND 8-15]
**Visual**: New text card. Closer shot of hands on wheel.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You do NOT have to consent to a search. If the officer asks, 'Do you mind if I look in your car?' the answer is: 'I do not consent to a search.' Say it clearly. Say it calmly. This is a constitutional right, not disrespect."
**Text overlay**: "'I do not consent to a search.' Constitutional right, not disrespect."

[SECOND 15-22]
**Visual**: New text card.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You do NOT have to perform field sobriety tests in most states. These are voluntary. They're designed to generate evidence against you. You can decline. The breathalyzer is more complicated — refusal has consequences in most states — but field sobriety tests are different."
**Text overlay**: "Field sobriety tests: voluntary in most states. Breathalyzer: refusal has consequences."

[SECOND 22-27]
**Visual**: POV hands on wheel. Officer walks away. Dashboard visible.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If you're arrested, invoke your rights clearly: 'I want an attorney before answering any questions.' Then stop talking. Everything after that line is protected."
**Text overlay**: "'I want an attorney before answering any questions.' Then stop."

**Visual**: Rearview mirror — the lights are gone. Road is clear ahead.
**Text overlay**: "Know your rights BEFORE you need them. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Engine hum. Quiet confidence. Beat resolves.
