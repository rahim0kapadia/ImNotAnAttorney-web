# TikTok Script #25: The Collateral Consequences Nobody Lists on the Plea Agreement

**Topic:** Hidden lifelong consequences of pleading guilty
**Format:** talking head + cascading text overlay + reveal
**Duration:** 45s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + building dread beat with each reveal

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A plea agreement on a table. The signature line is highlighted. Above it, small print is zoomed into -- but it's blurry, unreadable.
**Text overlay**: "The plea agreement lists your sentence. It doesn't list everything else you lose."
**Audio**: Pen tapping on table.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, direct eye contact.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "When you plead guilty, you're not just accepting a sentence. You're accepting a list of consequences that follows you for the rest of your life. And most attorneys don't walk you through all of them."

## Body
[SECOND 3-15]
**Visual**: Each consequence drops onto screen like a weight falling. Heavy. Impactful. One every 2 seconds.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Criminal record -- permanent. Job applications -- every single one asks. Housing -- landlords run background checks. Professional licenses -- revoked or denied. Custody implications -- used against you in family court. Student loans -- certain convictions disqualify you. Immigration -- even legal residents can face deportation. Gun rights -- gone. Voting rights -- restricted in some states."
**Text overlay**: Each consequence drops as spoken: "CRIMINAL RECORD. JOBS. HOUSING. LICENSES. CUSTODY. STUDENT LOANS. IMMIGRATION. GUN RIGHTS. VOTING."

[SECOND 15-25]
**Visual**: All consequences remain on screen. Speaker appears in a corner window.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The sentence on the plea agreement is just the beginning. The judge might give you probation. Great. But the record follows you to every job interview, every apartment application, every custody hearing, every professional licensing board for years -- sometimes decades -- sometimes forever. This is what 'collateral consequences' means. And most attorneys never bring it up."
**Text overlay**: "The sentence ends. The consequences don't."

[SECOND 25-35]
**Visual**: Questions appearing on screen, clean and urgent.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Before you sign any plea, ask your attorney these questions: what are the specific collateral consequences of this plea? Will this affect my immigration status? Can this be expunged in the future? Is there a diversion program that avoids a conviction on my record? And -- is there a lesser charge we can negotiate that carries fewer long-term consequences?"
**Text overlay**: Questions appearing as spoken.

[SECOND 35-42]
**Visual**: The plea agreement from the opening -- but now the blurry fine print is in focus, showing a handwritten list of all the consequences, with "DISCUSSED WITH CLIENT" circled and unsigned.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "A plea deal might be the right decision. But it should be an informed decision. Not a panicked one. Not a pressured one. An informed one. With full knowledge of everything you're accepting -- not just the sentence."
**Text overlay**: "Informed decision. Not pressured. Not panicked. Informed."

[SECOND 42-45]
**Visual**: Clean black screen.
**Text overlay**: "Know what you're signing. Know What They Know. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Beat resolves to silence.

Save this. You need to see this list before your next court date.

## Caption
The plea agreement lists your sentence. It doesn't list everything else: criminal record, job applications, housing, custody, immigration, professional licenses, student loans, gun rights, voting. Most attorneys never walk you through the full list. Ask before you sign.
