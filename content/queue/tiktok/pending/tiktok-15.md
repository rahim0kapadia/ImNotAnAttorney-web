# TikTok Script #15: 3 Questions That Scare Lazy Attorneys

**Topic:** Accountability questions that reveal whether your attorney is working
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + dramatic delivery
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio, voiceover + confident trap beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A phone showing 8 missed calls to "My Attorney." Phone gets set down. Person turns to camera.
**Text overlay**: "3 questions that scare lazy attorneys."
**Audio**: Phone ring cutting off abruptly.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, slight smile, direct eye contact. Controlled confidence.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If your attorney can answer these without flinching, you've got a good one. If they can't, now you know."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: "QUESTION 1" appears with dramatic weight. Text fills in as spoken.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question one: 'What motions have you FILED, not considered, not planned, actually filed?' Any attorney can say they're 'considering' a suppression motion. The court docket shows what's been filed. If nothing is on the docket, nothing has been done. That's not strategy. That's stalling."
**Text overlay**: "Q1: What motions have you FILED? Check the docket."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: "QUESTION 2" appears.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question two: 'What are the three weakest points in the prosecution's case against me?' A prepared attorney can rattle these off. An unprepared one will give you a vague answer about 'needing to review the file more.' If they don't know the prosecution's weaknesses, they can't exploit them."
**Text overlay**: "Q2: What are the 3 weakest points in the prosecution's case?"

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: "QUESTION 3" appears.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question three: 'If this were your family member's case, would you recommend this approach?' Watch their face. A good attorney will engage seriously with this question. A bad one will deflect. This question cuts through the professional distance and asks them to be honest about whether they're actually fighting."
**Text overlay**: "Q3: If this were YOUR family member, would you recommend this?"

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: The phone from the opening, but now it shows a text TO the attorney with all three questions typed out, ready to send.
**Text overlay**: "Send these today. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Confident beat drops.

## CTA
Follow for more questions that hold attorneys accountable.
