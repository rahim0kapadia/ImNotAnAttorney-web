[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to person, eyebrows raised.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Your case has been going on for over a year. Your attorney says it's strategy. Let's talk about when it is and when it isn't."

[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Split screen. Left: "Strategic continuance" in green. Right: "Scheduling continuance" in red.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "A strategic continuance comes with a specific reason. Waiting for a witness. Waiting for a ruling. Waiting for lab results. A scheduling continuance comes with a vague answer. 'We need more time.' Ask: more time to do what? If nothing has been filed between continuances, the delay isn't strategy. It's calendar management."
**Text overlay**: "Strategic = specific reason. Scheduling = vague answer."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Text appearing on screen.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The question that exposes everything: 'What specific work was completed since our last court date?' A strategic attorney has an answer. A scheduling attorney doesn't."
**Text overlay**: "THE QUESTION: What work was completed since our last date?"

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Timeline showing 14 months. Each month labeled with either a filing (green) or just a continuance (red). The graphic makes the pattern obvious.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Meanwhile, you're losing income. Losing sleep. Losing relationships. Every month your case drags is a month of your life on hold. You deserve to know why."
**Text overlay**: "Every month on hold is a month of your life."

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Person checking their phone — opening a court docket lookup.
**Text overlay**: "Check the filings between continuances. That's your answer. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Clock stops. Beat resolves.
