# TikTok Script #7: Your Attorney's "Trust Me" Is a Red Flag

**Topic:** Red flags that your attorney isn't doing their job
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + warning sign visuals
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + alarm-style ambient beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Text bubble animation: "Just trust me. I've been doing this for 20 years."
**Text overlay**: "If your attorney says this instead of explaining, that's a red flag."
**Audio**: Text message pop sound. Then silence.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, arms crossed, direct eye contact.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Experience is not an answer. An answer is an answer."

## Body
[SECOND 3-10]
**Visual**: Red flag icon drops in. "RED FLAG #1" banner.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You ask about filing a motion. They say 'that won't work.' You ask why. They say 'trust me.' A good attorney explains why a motion won't succeed. A bad one doesn't think they owe you an explanation. Your freedom is on the line. You're owed an explanation."
**Text overlay**: "RED FLAG: 'Trust me' instead of an actual explanation"

[SECOND 10-17]
**Visual**: Red flag #2 drops in.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You ask for a case update. They say 'we're working on it.' You ask what specifically they've done. Silence. Or 'it's complicated.' If your attorney can't tell you what they've done on your case this month, the answer might be nothing."
**Text overlay**: "RED FLAG: 'We're working on it' with no specifics"

[SECOND 17-24]
**Visual**: Red flag #3 drops in.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "They can't remember the details of YOUR case. If they have to check the file to remember your charges, confuse you with another client, or ask questions they should already know -- you're not being represented. You're being processed."
**Text overlay**: "RED FLAG: Confuses your case details or charges"

[SECOND 24-30]
**Visual**: All three red flags on screen. Then they transform into three green checkmark questions.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask instead: what is your theory of defense? What motions have you considered? What are the prosecution's weaknesses?"
**Text overlay**: "Replace red flags with real questions. imnotanattorney.com/score"
**Audio**: Alert sound transitions to confident beat.
