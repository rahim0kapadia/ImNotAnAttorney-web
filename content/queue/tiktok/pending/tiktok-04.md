# TikTok Script #4: 97% of Federal Cases Never Go to Trial

**Topic:** The plea machine and trial penalty
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + data visual
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + ominous ambient drone

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A large "97%" fills the entire screen in white text on black. Slowly zooms in.
**Text overlay**: "97% of federal cases end in a plea deal."
**Audio**: Low rumble. Building tension.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, controlled intensity.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ninety-seven percent. That means for every 100 people charged in federal court, only 3 ever see a jury."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Visual of 100 figures on screen. 97 of them dim out, leaving 3 standing.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "This is not because 97% of people are guilty. It's because the system is built to make a trial the most expensive decision you'll ever make. It's called the trial penalty -- and it's the most powerful force in American criminal justice that nobody puts in writing."
**Text overlay**: "The trial penalty. Nobody puts it in writing."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Side-by-side comparison chart. "PLEA: 2 years" vs. "TRIAL LOSS: 12 years"
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If you go to trial and lose, your sentence will be three to six times harsher than the plea. That's documented by researchers who've studied this for decades. So exercising your constitutional right to a trial comes with a price tag measured in years of your freedom."
**Text overlay**: "Plea: 2 years. Trial loss: 6-12 years. Same crime."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, softer tone now.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "This isn't telling you to take or reject any deal. It's telling you to ask one question before you decide: if I reject this offer and we go to trial and lose, what is the likely sentencing difference? Your attorney should be able to give you a specific number."
**Text overlay**: "ASK: What's the specific sentencing difference between plea and trial loss?"

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: The 97% from the opening -- but now one of the dimmed figures lights back up, holding a notepad.
**Text overlay**: "Know the math before you sign. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Drone resolves to silence.
