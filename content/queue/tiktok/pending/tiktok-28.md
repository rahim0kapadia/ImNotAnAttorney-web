# TikTok Script #28: Your Attorney Missed a Deadline. Now What?

**Topic:** What happens when an attorney misses a critical filing deadline
**Format:** storytime + text overlay + action framework
**Duration:** 45s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + urgent-then-resolved beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A court docket printout on screen. One line is highlighted in red: "MOTION TO SUPPRESS -- DEADLINE: EXPIRED." Next to it, the motions column is blank.
**Text overlay**: "Your attorney missed the deadline. That defense option is gone forever."
**Audio**: Paper crumpling. Heavy bass.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, controlled frustration.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Deadlines in criminal cases aren't suggestions. Some of them, once missed, close permanently. And your attorney might not even tell you it happened."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Timeline showing three types of deadlines -- each with consequences listed.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Suppression motions: typically 30 to 45 days after arraignment. Miss it, you waive the right to challenge how evidence was obtained. Speedy trial demands: some states allow you to demand trial within a certain period, but only if the demand is made on time. Brady requests: the prosecution must disclose evidence favorable to you, but your attorney has to formally request it. None of these file themselves."
**Text overlay**: "Suppression: 30-45 days. Speedy trial: specific window. Brady: must be requested."

[SECOND 12-22]
**Visual**: Two paths. "WHAT YOU CAN DO" heading.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If your attorney missed a deadline, here's what you can do. First: document it. Pull the court docket and verify what was or wasn't filed. Second: ask your attorney directly -- why wasn't this filed? Was there a strategic reason, or was it oversight? Their answer matters. Third: if it was negligence, you may have grounds for a motion claiming ineffective assistance of counsel. Fourth: consider whether a bar complaint is appropriate. Fifth: consult a second attorney about your options."
**Text overlay**: "1. Document it (pull the docket). 2. Ask why. 3. Ineffective assistance motion? 4. Bar complaint? 5. Second opinion."

[SECOND 22-32]
**Visual**: Strickland v. Washington standard appearing on screen.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The legal standard for ineffective assistance comes from Strickland v. Washington. You have to show two things: your attorney's performance fell below an objective standard of reasonableness, AND the deficiency prejudiced your case -- meaning there's a reasonable probability the outcome would have been different. That's a high bar, but missed deadlines with provable consequences are exactly the kind of thing it covers."
**Text overlay**: "Strickland: (1) Fell below standard + (2) Prejudiced the outcome. High bar -- but missed deadlines qualify."

[SECOND 32-40]
**Visual**: Questions appearing in list format.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your attorney right now: what deadlines apply to my case? Which ones have already passed? Which ones are coming up? And what has been filed versus what's been discussed?"
**Text overlay**: "ASK TODAY: What deadlines apply? Which passed? Which are coming? What's been FILED?"

[SECOND 40-45]
**Visual**: The court docket from the opening -- but now showing a new entry: "MOTION FOR RELIEF -- FILED."
**Text overlay**: "A missed deadline isn't the end. But silence is. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Urgent beat resolves to confident.
