## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Text appearing -- Question 1 with dramatic weight.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question one: what specific evidence does the government have that I intended to defraud anyone? Intent is the heart of every wire fraud case. The government has to prove you KNEW what you were saying was false at the time you said it. Business deals go sideways every day. That's not automatically fraud. If your attorney can't tell you the government's evidence for intent, they haven't done the work."
**Text overlay**: "Q1: What evidence do they have for INTENT? Business failure is not fraud."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Question 2 appearing.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question two: can we argue good faith belief? If you genuinely believed your representations were true -- even if they turned out to be wrong -- that negates intent. Period. Your attorney should be gathering every email, every plan, every action inconsistent with someone running a scam."
**Text overlay**: "Q2: Good faith belief? If you believed it was true, that negates intent."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Question 3 appearing.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Question three: have you reviewed ALL discovery and what's the most damaging piece? Federal wire fraud cases generate thousands of documents. Some attorneys drown in it. Some skim it. Neither is acceptable when you're facing decades."
**Text overlay**: "Q3: Have you reviewed ALL discovery? What's the most damaging piece?"
