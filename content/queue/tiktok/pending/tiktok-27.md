# TikTok Script #27: Ask Your Attorney If the Search Was Legal

**Topic:** Fourth Amendment search and seizure -- was the stop/search valid?
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + quick-hit empowerment
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + authoritative beat with legal weight

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Dashcam footage perspective -- a car being pulled over. Red and blue lights in the rearview mirror.
**Text overlay**: "The officer pulled you over. But did they have a legal reason?"
**Audio**: Siren whoop. Engine idling.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, direct and calm.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If the stop was illegal, everything that followed is fruit of the poisonous tree. The drugs. The breathalyzer. The confession. All of it."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Legal flowchart: "Was the stop legal?" > YES/NO. If NO > "Evidence = suppressible."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The Fourth Amendment protects you from unreasonable searches and seizures. If the officer didn't have reasonable suspicion for the traffic stop, the stop itself is invalid. And if the stop is invalid, the search that followed is invalid. The evidence found during that search? Potentially inadmissible. This is what a suppression motion is for -- and it can end your case entirely."
**Text overlay**: "Illegal stop = illegal search = inadmissible evidence = case can end."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: List of common reasons stops are challenged.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Common challenges: the officer said you crossed the center line but the dashcam shows you didn't. They said you were 'driving erratically' but can't specify how. They pulled you over for a broken taillight but your taillight wasn't broken. The stated reason for the stop doesn't match the evidence -- and in DUI and drug cases, that's where your defense might live."
**Text overlay**: "Dashcam vs. police report. Do they match?"

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Questions appearing on screen.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your attorney: what was the officer's stated reason for the stop? Does the dashcam or body cam support that reason? Has a suppression motion been considered? What's the deadline for filing one?"
**Text overlay**: "ASK: Stated reason? Does dashcam support it? Suppression motion considered? Deadline?"

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Dashcam footage from the opening -- but now a text overlay appears on the footage: "MOTION TO SUPPRESS -- FILED."
**Text overlay**: "If the stop was bad, the case might be too. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Siren fades. Confident beat.

## CTA
Save this -- especially if your case started with a traffic stop.
