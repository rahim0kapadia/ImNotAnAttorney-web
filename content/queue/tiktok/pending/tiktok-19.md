# TikTok Script #19: Nobody Told Me I Could Request New Counsel

**Topic:** Defendants' right to request new counsel
**Format:** storytime + text overlay + empowerment
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + building empowerment beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: A person sitting in a courtroom gallery, looking defeated. Head in hands. Their public defender is at the front talking to the judge about someone else's case.
**Text overlay**: "Nobody told me I could get a new attorney."
**Audio**: Courtroom murmur. Gavel in background.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, direct.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "You can fire your attorney. You can request new counsel. These are real options the system counts on you not knowing."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Two paths showing on screen. "PRIVATE ATTORNEY" path: "Terminate at any time. It's your right." "PUBLIC DEFENDER" path: "File a motion for substitution of counsel."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "If you have a private attorney, you can terminate them at any time. They work for you. Get a new one first, then fire the old one. If you have a public defender, you can file a motion for substitution of counsel. You'll need to show a breakdown in the attorney-client relationship -- and the bar for that is not as high as most judges make it sound."
**Text overlay**: "Private: fire at any time. Public defender: motion for substitution."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Evidence list of what constitutes "breakdown" -- appearing one at a time.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "What counts as a breakdown? Failure to communicate for extended periods. Failure to review discovery. Conflict of interest. Refusing to file motions without explanation. Making decisions about your case without your consent. These aren't personality conflicts. These are professional failures."
**Text overlay**: "Breakdown = no communication, no discovery review, no motions, decisions without consent."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, warmer tone.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Before you make the switch, document everything. Communication logs. Unanswered calls. Missed deadlines. What motions weren't filed. Then consult a new attorney before dropping the current one. Never be without representation during an active case."
**Text overlay**: "Document first. Consult new attorney. Then make the switch."

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: The courtroom from the opening -- but now the person is standing, notepad in hand, walking toward the clerk's window.
**Text overlay**: "You have options the system doesn't advertise. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Confident beat. Footsteps.

Follow for more rights nobody tells defendants about.
