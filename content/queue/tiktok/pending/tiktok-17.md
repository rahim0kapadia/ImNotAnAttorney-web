# TikTok Script #17: The Communication Standard Your Attorney Is Supposed to Meet

**Topic:** ABA communication standards for attorney-client relationship
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + escalation framework
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio, voiceover + measured, authoritative beat

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Call log showing 12 outgoing calls to "Attorney's Office." All unanswered. Dates spanning 3 weeks.
**Text overlay**: "12 calls. 3 weeks. Zero callbacks."
**Audio**: Phone ringing out. Voicemail beep. Silence.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, calm but firm.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Your attorney has a professional obligation to communicate with you. This is not a favor. It's a rule."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: ABA Model Rule 1.4 text appearing on screen, key phrases highlighted.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "ABA Model Rule 1.4. Your attorney must promptly inform you of decisions requiring your consent. Must explain matters to the extent needed for you to make informed decisions. Must respond to reasonable requests for information. Most state bars adopt this rule or something nearly identical. Two to three weeks of silence without a proactive update is not 'busy.' It's a pattern."
**Text overlay**: "ABA Rule 1.4: Attorneys MUST keep clients informed. This is not optional."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Escalation ladder appearing, step by step.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Here's the escalation. Step one: documented voicemail with specific questions and a response deadline. Step two: email repeating those questions, paper trail. Step three: certified letter requesting a case status update within 48 hours. Step four: state bar complaint for communication failure."
**Text overlay**: "1. Voicemail with deadline. 2. Email (paper trail). 3. Certified letter. 4. Bar complaint."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Close-up of an email being typed. The key line highlighted: "I'm documenting all communications for my file."
**Audio/Voiceover**: "One sentence changes the dynamic. In your next voicemail or email, say this: 'I'm documenting all communications for my file.' Watch how fast they call back. That sentence tells them you know your rights and you're building a record."
**Text overlay**: "'I'm documenting all communications for my file.' Say this."

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Call log from the opening, but now showing an incoming call from "Attorney's Office" with a green answer button.
**Text overlay**: "You have more power than you think. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Phone rings. Confident beat.

## CTA
Follow for the words that actually get your attorney to call back.
