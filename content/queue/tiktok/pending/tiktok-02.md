# TikTok Script #2: Your Public Defender Has 7 Minutes for Your Case

**Topic:** Public defender caseload crisis
**Format:** talking head + text overlay + math visual
**Duration:** 30s
**Trending sound suggestion:** Original audio -- voiceover + ticking clock ambient

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
[SECOND 0-1]
**Visual**: Calculator app on screen. Someone types: 50 / 400 = 0.125. The number "7.5 minutes" appears in bold below.
**Text overlay**: "Your public defender has 7 minutes per week for your case."
**Audio**: Calculator tap sounds. Then silence.

[SECOND 1-3]
**Visual**: Cut to speaker, dead serious.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "That's not an insult. That's math."

## Body
[SECOND 3-12]
**Visual**: Text breakdown appearing on screen like a whiteboard calculation.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "The ABA recommends a maximum of 150 felony cases per attorney per year. Most public defenders are carrying 300 to 500 active cases. At 400 cases and a 50-hour week, each client gets seven and a half minutes. That's not enough time to read a police report, let alone prepare a suppression motion."
**Text overlay**: "ABA max: 150 cases. Reality: 300-500 cases. Your share: 7.5 min/week."

[SECOND 12-20]
**Visual**: Cut to closer angle. Tone shifts from informational to empowering.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "This is not a failure of your public defender. Many of them are brilliant attorneys doing impossible work. This is a failure of how we fund criminal defense in this country. But knowing this changes what YOU do. You can't just trust the process. You have to be involved."
**Text overlay**: "Not bad attorneys. Broken system. YOU have to be involved."

[SECOND 20-27]
**Visual**: Text list of questions appearing.
**Audio/Voiceover**: "Ask your public defender three things. How many active cases are you handling? How much time have you spent on my case this week? What would you do differently if you had more time?"
**Text overlay**: Questions appear as spoken.

[SECOND 27-30]
**Visual**: Calculator from the opening -- but now someone types a different equation: "YOUR QUESTIONS = MORE TIME"
**Text overlay**: "Make those 7 minutes count. imnotanattorney.com"
**Audio**: Clock stops ticking.

## CTA
Follow for more questions that hold the system accountable.
