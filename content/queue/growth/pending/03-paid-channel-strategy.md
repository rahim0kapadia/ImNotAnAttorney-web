# Paid Channel Strategy with Budget Recommendations: ImNotAnAttorney

**Created:** 2026-03-11
**Goal:** Identify the highest-ROI paid channels for INNA, set realistic budgets, and define a testing sequence that minimizes waste.

---

## Strategic Context

INNA is NOT a law firm. This fundamentally changes the paid advertising equation:

1. **We are not competing for "$50-$150 per click" criminal defense keywords.** Law firms bid on "criminal defense lawyer near me" because a single client is worth $5K-$100K. We sell $97 playbooks and $197 reports. We cannot afford $50+ clicks.

2. **Our funnel has a low entry point ($97) with an upgrade path to $9,997.** This means we can afford higher CAC on initial acquisition if the upgrade path works. But we need to prove the upgrade path first before committing to expensive channels.

3. **Our audience is in emotional crisis.** They just got arrested, or they just found out their attorney isn't working. They are searching NOW. This means high-intent keywords work, but also means we can catch them in non-search contexts (social, content discovery).

4. **We have strong free content.** 29 blog posts, a free score quiz, and a free discovery checklist. We can use paid channels to drive traffic to free content and convert downstream, not just direct-to-purchase.

INNA cannot compete on head terms. "Criminal defense lawyer" costs $35-$50+ per click. "DUI lawyer near me" is $75-$140. These are law firm keywords -- the CPCs reflect the lifetime value of a legal client ($5K-$100K), not a $97 playbook buyer.

| Attorney accountability | "my lawyer won't call me back" | $1-$5 | High -- frustrated defendant |
| Attorney accountability | "is my lawyer doing anything on my case" | $1-$5 | High -- suspicious defendant |
| Attorney accountability | "how to know if your attorney is working" | $2-$8 | High -- maps to our blog |
| Discovery questions | "how to read discovery documents" | $1-$5 | Medium -- research stage |
| Discovery questions | "what should be in my discovery" | $1-$5 | Medium -- maps to our blog |
| Defense questions | "questions to ask criminal defense attorney" | $3-$10 | High -- maps to our core product |
| Defense questions | "what motions should my attorney file" | $2-$8 | High -- maps to our blog |
| Process questions | "what happens at arraignment" | $2-$6 | Medium -- early stage defendant |
| Process questions | "first time felony what to expect" | $2-$6 | Medium -- maps to our blog |
| DUI specific | "can DUI be dismissed" | $5-$15 | High -- maps to our blog + playbook |
| DUI specific | "DUI defense questions" | $3-$10 | High -- maps to our playbook |

**Why these keywords work:**

- Every keyword maps directly to an existing INNA blog post or product
- We are the best answer for these searches -- no law firm can answer "is my lawyer doing anything" honestly

**Google Ads Budget Recommendation:**

**Google Ads Verdict:** START HERE. Best channel for high-intent traffic. Long-tail keywords let us compete without law firm budgets. Every keyword maps to existing content.

### Channel 2: Reddit Ads

**Strategy: Subreddit Targeting with Educational Content**

Reddit Ads are significantly cheaper than Google ($0.50-$4.00 CPC, $3.50-$15.00 CPM) and allow subreddit-specific targeting. This means we can put INNA content directly in front of the exact communities our organic Reddit strategy targets.

**Target subreddits for ads:**
- r/legaladvice (2.5M+ members)
- r/dui (35K+ members)
- r/personalfinance (when targeting "legal costs" interest)
- r/AskReddit (broad, but cheap CPM)
- Interest-based targeting: "criminal defense," "legal advice," "DUI"

**Promoted Posts (best for INNA):**
These blend with organic content. For legal audiences, educational ads outperform sales ads.

**Reddit Ads Budget Recommendation:**

|-------|---------------|----------|------|

**Important:** Reddit ads can run in PARALLEL with the organic Reddit strategy (Phase 1-4 SOP). The ads come from a different account (brand account) than the organic presence (personal account). They complement each other.

### Channel 3: Facebook / Instagram Ads

**Strategy: Pain-Point Creative Targeting Defendant Demographics**

Facebook/Instagram cannot directly target "criminals" or "defendants" (Meta restricts targeting based on legal status). Instead, target based on:

**Targeting approach:**
- **Interest-based:** Criminal justice system, legal aid, bail bonds, public defender, criminal defense attorney, court system
- **Behavioral:** Recently searched legal topics (Meta cross-references search behavior)
- **Life events:** None directly applicable, but financial stress indicators correlate
- **Lookalike audiences:** Build from email subscriber list (once it reaches 100+ contacts)
- **Geographic:** Target metro areas with high crime rates and active court systems
- **Demographic:** Age 18-45, all genders, broader income ranges (defendants span all demographics)

**Ad creative that works:**

Creative 1 -- Pain Point (Video):

**Facebook/Instagram Budget Recommendation:**

| Phase | Monthly Budget | Duration | Goal |
|-------|---------------|----------|------|

- CTR: 1-2%
- $500/month = ~165-500 clicks
- Landing page conversion: 5-10% (lower than search -- cold traffic)

**Facebook/Instagram Verdict:** TEST THIRD. Best for awareness and retargeting, not first purchase. Use primarily for:
1. Retargeting website visitors who didn't convert (cheapest, highest ROI use)

- Score quiz visitors who didn't purchase (show Playbook ad)
- Blog readers who didn't take the quiz (show Score quiz ad)
- Checkout page visitors who didn't complete purchase (show urgency/guarantee ad)

Retargeting CPCs are typically $0.50-$1.00 and conversion rates are 3-5x higher than cold traffic.

## Recommended Testing Sequence

### Month 1-2: Google Ads (long-tail keywords)
- **Budget:** $500-$1,000/month
- **Why first:** Highest intent traffic. People actively searching for answers INNA provides.
- **Landing pages:** Blog posts (matching keyword intent) + Score quiz
- **Success metric:** Cost per email capture under $30. Cost per $97 sale under $100.
- **Install:** Meta Pixel, Reddit Pixel (for future retargeting even before running ads on those platforms)

### Month 2-3: Reddit Ads (add to Google)
- **Budget:** $500-$1,000/month (total across both channels: $1,000-$2,000)
- **Why second:** Cheapest clicks, direct subreddit targeting, complements organic Reddit strategy.
- **Landing pages:** Score quiz (primary), blog posts (secondary)
- **Success metric:** Cost per score quiz completion under $5. Cost per email capture under $15.

### Month 3-4: Facebook/Instagram Retargeting (add to Google + Reddit)
- **Budget:** $300-$500/month for retargeting only
- **Why third:** By now, website has visitor data from Google + Reddit traffic. Retargeting is the highest-ROI paid tactic.
- **Target:** Website visitors who didn't convert, score quiz visitors who didn't buy
- **Success metric:** Retargeting conversion rate 3-5x higher than cold traffic. Cost per conversion under $50.

### Month 4-5: Facebook/Instagram Cold (add to retargeting)
- **Budget:** $500-$1,000/month
- **Why fourth:** Use creative that worked in retargeting for cold audiences. Test founder story video, pain point carousel, stat hooks.
- **Success metric:** Cost per email capture under $25. Cost per $97 sale under $150.

### Month 5-6: YouTube Pre-Roll (if budget allows)
- **Budget:** $300-$500/month
- **Why fifth:** Awareness layer. Reinforces brand for people already seeing INNA through other channels.
- **Success metric:** View completion rate above 40%. Branded search increase after campaign start.

**Critical insight:** The upgrade path means initial CAC is front-loaded. A customer acquired for $50 who buys a $97 Playbook and later upgrades to a $197 Case Decoder has an LTV of $294. If they upgrade to the $997 Intelligence Brief, LTV is $997 (credit applied, but revenue is $997 total). The $50 CAC against $997 LTV is a 20:1 ratio -- exceptional.

**This means:** Even if Month 1-2 CAC on Playbooks looks tight ($50-$150 CAC on $97 product), the upgrade path makes it profitable. The key metric is not first-purchase CAC but 90-day LTV.

## Conversion Tracking Setup (Required Before Spending)

Before launching any paid channel, install these tracking mechanisms:

### Google Analytics 4
- Track: page views, score quiz completions, email captures, checkout starts, purchases
- Set up conversion events for each step of the funnel
- Enable enhanced ecommerce tracking for Stripe checkout

### Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram)
- Install on ALL pages of imnotanattorney.com
- Track: PageView, ViewContent (blog/score/sample), Lead (email capture), InitiateCheckout, Purchase
- Build custom audiences from pixel data for retargeting

### Reddit Pixel
- Install sitewide
- Track: PageVisit, ViewContent, Lead, Purchase
- Build retargeting audiences from Reddit traffic

### UTM Parameter Discipline
Every paid link must include:
```
?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[ad_type]&utm_campaign=[campaign_name]&utm_content=[ad_variant]
```
Examples:
- `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=attorney-accountability&utm_content=ad-v1`
- `?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=promoted_post&utm_campaign=score-quiz&utm_content=ad-v2`
- `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=retargeting&utm_campaign=blog-readers&utm_content=carousel-v1`

### Attribution Model
Use last-click attribution for direct-response channels (Google, Reddit direct-to-purchase) and first-click attribution for awareness channels (Facebook cold, YouTube). Report both models weekly during testing phase.

## Weekly Optimization Checklist (During Active Campaigns)

- [ ] Review CPC by keyword/ad (pause anything over 2x target CPC)
- [ ] Review CTR by ad (pause anything under 1% CTR)
- [ ] Review landing page conversion rate (A/B test if under 10%)
- [ ] Check negative keywords (add irrelevant search terms to negative list)
- [ ] Review cost per email capture (target: under $30)
- [ ] Review cost per quiz completion (target: under $5)
- [ ] Review cost per purchase (target: under $100 for Playbooks, under $200 for Case Decoder)
- [ ] Check budget pacing (not running out mid-month or underspending)
- [ ] Review day-of-week and time-of-day performance (shift budget to best windows)
- [ ] Check geographic performance (some metros will convert better)

## Channels NOT Recommended (And Why)

### Google LSA (Local Service Ads)
INNA is not a licensed attorney/law firm. LSA requires practice-area verification.

### TikTok Ads
Audience demographics skew young (18-24). Criminal defendants span all ages and the highest-value cases (felonies, federal) skew 25-55. TikTok organic content is worth testing, but paid ads are not recommended until organic TikTok proves the audience is there.

### LinkedIn Ads
Wrong audience. Criminal defendants do not research their cases on LinkedIn. White collar defendants might, but the CPCs ($5-$15) and CPM ($30-$80) make it too expensive for $97 products.

### Print/Radio/TV
Too expensive, too broad, no tracking. INNA needs precision targeting, not mass awareness.

### Bing Ads
Low volume for legal queries. Worth testing eventually (Bing CPCs are 30-50% cheaper than Google with similar intent) but not in the first 6 months when budget is limited.

## Key Decisions Before Launching

1. **Set a hard monthly cap.** Do not exceed budget under any circumstances during testing.
2. **Define "kill criteria."** If a channel does not produce at least 1 email capture per $50 spent within 14 days, pause and diagnose before spending more.
3. **Commit to 90 days minimum** on Google and Reddit before declaring a channel "doesn't work." Paid channels require optimization time.
4. **Install ALL tracking pixels before spending a single dollar.** Retargeting data is too valuable to miss.
5. **Separate brand search from non-brand search** in Google Ads reporting. Brand searches ("imnotanattorney") will skew metrics and should be in their own campaign.
