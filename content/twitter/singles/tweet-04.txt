# Single Tweet: What Motions

Draft Status: Ready

"What motions have you filed?"

Four words. Your attorney's entire answer reveals everything.

"I've filed X, Y, and Z because..." = Working attorney.

"We don't need any motions." = Lazy attorney.

"We'll see what happens." = No strategy.

Ask the question. Write down the answer.
