# Single Tweet: The Processing vs Defense

Draft Status: Ready

---

Your attorney processes your case.

Or your attorney defends your case.

Processing = Show up → Request continuance → Repeat → Plea deal

Defense = Investigate → File motions → Challenge evidence → Fight

The difference? Questions.

Ask what they're doing. Every time.
