577.010. Driving while intoxicated. — 1. A person commits the offense of driving while intoxicated if he or she operates a vehicle while in an intoxicated condition.

2. The offense of driving while intoxicated is:

(1) A class B misdemeanor;

(2) A class A misdemeanor if the defendant is a prior offender;

(3) A class E felony for a persistent offender. See section 577.023 for definitions.

3. No person convicted of or pleading guilty to driving while intoxicated shall be granted a suspended imposition of sentence.

(L. 1982 S.B. 513, A.L. 2014 H.B. 1371)

Effective 1-1-17
