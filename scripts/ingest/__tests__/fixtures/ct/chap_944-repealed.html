Sec. 53a-100. Definitions. As used in this part: (1) "Building" in addition to its ordinary meaning, includes any watercraft, aircraft, trailer, sleeping car, railroad car or other structure or vehicle.

Sec. 53a-101. Burglary in the first degree: Class B felony.

(a) A person is guilty of burglary in the first degree when such person enters or remains unlawfully in a building with intent to commit a crime therein and: (1) Such person is armed with explosives or a deadly weapon or dangerous instrument, or (2) in the course of committing the offense, such person intentionally, knowingly or recklessly inflicts or attempts to inflict bodily injury on anyone.

Sec. 53a-103a. Repealed. Section 53a-103a is repealed, effective October 1, 2010. (P.A. 10-32, S. 178.)
