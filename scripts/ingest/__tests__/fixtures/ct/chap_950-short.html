Chapter 950

PENAL CODE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

Sec. 53a-1. Short title: Penal Code.

Sec. 53a-2. Applicability.

Sec. 53a-3. Definitions.

Sec. 53a-1. Short title: Penal Code. This title shall be known and may be cited as the "Penal Code."

Sec. 53a-2. Applicability. The provisions of this title shall apply to any offense defined in this title or the general statutes, unless otherwise expressly provided or unless the context otherwise requires, committed on or after October 1, 1971.
