CHAPTER 1. Homicide [187 - 199]
( Chapter 1 enacted 1872. )

187.

(a) Murder is the unlawful killing of a human being, or a fetus, with malice aforethought.

(b) This section shall not apply to any person who commits an act that results in the death of a fetus if any of the following apply:

(1) The act complied with the former Therapeutic Abortion Act.

(2) The act was solicited, aided, abetted, or consented to by the mother of the fetus.

(Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 385.)
