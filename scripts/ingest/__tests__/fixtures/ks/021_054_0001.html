21-5401. Capital murder. (a) Capital murder is the:

(1) Intentional and premeditated killing of any person in the commission of kidnapping, as defined in K.S.A. 21-5408(a), and amendments thereto, when there is the intent to hold such person for ransom;

(2) intentional and premeditated killing of any person pursuant to a contract or agreement to kill such person or being a party to the contract or agreement pursuant to which such person is killed;

(3) intentional and premeditated killing of any person by an inmate or prisoner confined in a state correctional institution;

(d) Capital murder is an off-grid person felony.

History: L. 2010, ch. 136, § 36; L. 2013, ch. 120, § 14; July 1.

Source or Prior Law: 21-3439.
