# ImNotAnAttorney (INAA) — Marketing Bible

## Brand Voice
Irreverent, bold, and unapologetically direct — written for people who are scared and need real
information fast. Think: confident paralegal friend who swears occasionally and doesn't sugarcoat.
Forbidden words/phrases: anything that sounds like a law firm pitch, anything condescending, vague
legalese. Every single piece of content — page, email, ad, social post — MUST include the UPL
disclaimer: "This is not legal advice. ImNotAnAttorney.com provides legal research tools for
educational purposes only."

## Target Audience
**Who buys:** Criminal defendants with active cases — DUI, drug possession, federal charges.
Decision moment is within 72 hours of arrest or first court date. Skews male, 25–50, some college.
Motivated by fear of outcome and distrust of expensive attorneys. Secondary buyer: family members
researching on behalf of a defendant (often the one paying).
**Who doesn't:** People with no active case (no urgency to buy). People who want a lawyer to just
handle it. Minors.

## Products & Pricing
- **Starter** $97 — state-specific case research report

## Constraints
- **UPL (Unauthorized Practice of Law):** Every piece of content must carry the disclaimer. Mary
  cannot approve or publish copy that omits it — no exceptions.
- No specific legal predictions in any content ("you will win," "charges will be dropped," "beats
  the case").
- Google and Meta ads can run but copy must never promise legal outcomes — requires copy review
  before launch.
- Audience is in crisis; humor that trivializes their situation is off-brand and harmful.
- No attorney referral language (we are not a referral service).
